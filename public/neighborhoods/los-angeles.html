The Story of
Los Angeles.

Los Angeles is a city of about 3,878,704 people in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1781. The name comes from Queen of Heaven. It goes by the nickname "City of Angels". For green space, Los Angeles has 360 parks, led by Ken Malloy Harbor Regional Park at roughly 316 acres. It has ocean beach: Cabrillo Beach, Royal Palms County Beach and Will Rogers State Beach. Open space and trails include Encino Reservoir and La Tuna Canyon Park.

Golfers have Los Angeles Country Club and Angeles National Golf Club. Local draws include Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, Dodger Stadium and Drake Stadium. California State University, Northridge is here too. The map shows 3544 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and Thai the most common. There are 691 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Los Angeles is the 58th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $928K for the typical home, and values are up 73% in ten years.

Los Angeles Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Los Angeles, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Los Angeles is worth about $927,891, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Los Angeles going up or down?

The typical home value in Los Angeles is down 0.5% over the past year, from about $932,759 to about $927,891. Over ten years it is up 73%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Los Angeles?

Typical values in Los Angeles by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $525,412, 2 bedroom about $731,545, 3 bedroom about $921,879, 4 bedroom about $1,175,024, 5+ bedroom about $1,990,832. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Los Angeles?

The typical asking rent in Los Angeles is about $2,772 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Los Angeles?

691 public schools sit inside the Los Angeles boundary: 470 elementary, 94 middle, 99 high and 28 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Los Angeles?

Los Angeles has 360 parks. The largest are Ken Malloy Harbor Regional Park (about 316 acres), Exposition Park (about 142 acres) and Pierce Farm Pastures and Meadows (about 97 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Los Angeles?

Los Angeles is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Inglewood, Lennox, Hawthorne and Westmont.
