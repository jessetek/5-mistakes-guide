The place

The Story of
Long Beach.

Long Beach is a city of about 450,901 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1888. There are 81 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Recreation Park, at about 178 acres. It has ocean beach: Long Beach City Beach, Mother's Beach and Alamitos Bay Beach. Open space and trails include Gull Park and Jack Dunster Marine Biological Reserve. Golfers have Skylinks Golf Course and Recreation Park 18 Golf Course.

Local draws include Veterans Memorial Stadium, Art Theatre and Edwards Long Beach Stadium 26 & IMAX. California State University, Long Beach is here too. The map shows 514 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and Pizza the most common. There are 70 public schools inside the boundary, run by Long Beach Unified. On price, Long Beach is the 49th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $852K for the typical home, and values are up 84% in ten years.

Why Long Beach

What Makes Long Beach
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🌊

Coastal Lifestyle

Beach access from $600K condos to $3M+ Naples Island homes. The ocean is part of everyday life here.

🏘️

Distinct Neighborhoods

Each Long Beach neighborhood has its own personality. Bixby Knolls is family-suburban, Belmont Shore is walkable-cool, Downtown is urban.

🍳

Brunch & Coffee Culture

From Steamed in Belmont Shore to Berlin Bistro in the East Village, Long Beach has one of the best food scenes in SoCal.

🎨

Arts & Music

The Pike Outlets, the Aquarium, the Convention Center, and a thriving live music scene make Long Beach LA's most cultural beach city.
