The place

The Story of
Costa Mesa.

About 109,131 people call Costa Mesa home. It sits in Orange County.

Its official start date is 1776, about 250 years ago. For green space, Costa Mesa has 29 parks, led by Fairview Park at roughly 216 acres. Golfers have Costa Mesa Country Club and Mesa Verde Country Club. Local draws include Harry R. LeBard Stadium, Orange County Museum of Art and Edwards Metro Pointe Stadium 12.

Orange Coast College is here too. The map shows 201 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and Japanese the most common. There are 18 public schools inside the boundary, run by Newport-Mesa Unified. On price, Costa Mesa is the 16th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.42M for the typical home, and values are up 111% in ten years.

Why Costa Mesa

What Makes Costa Mesa
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🍽️

Best Food Scene in OC

OC Mix, The Camp, Anaheim Street. Costa Mesa is where food entrepreneurs from LA and SF come to test concepts. That brings young creative buyers.

🛍️

South Coast Plaza Adjacency

Living within 10 minutes of the largest luxury mall in the US is a real lifestyle perk, and a steady value driver.

🏘️

Eastside Cottages

Costa Mesa's Eastside is full of restored 1950s-60s cottages on big lots. Distinct character, walkable to Newport Heights, premium-but-not-Newport pricing.

🎭

Segerstrom / Arts Adjacency

Segerstrom Center for the Arts and the Renee and Henry Segerstrom Concert Hall. OC's cultural backbone. Drives stable demand.

Top Areas

Where to Live
in Costa Mesa.

A real-talk breakdown of the Costa Mesa sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

Mesa Verde

Established mid-century family neighborhood with strong tree canopy and an active community. Costa Mesa's most stable sub-market.

📍

Eastside Costa Mesa

Cottage country. Restored 50s-60s homes, walkable to Newport Heights and 17th Street shopping.

📍

South Coast Metro

High-rise condos and townhomes near South Coast Plaza. Mostly investor + lifestyle buyers.

📍

Westside Costa Mesa

Industrial-arts district turning residential. Best value play, but watch traffic and zoning.
