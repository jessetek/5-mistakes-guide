The Story of
Monterey Park.

About 57,996 people call Monterey Park home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

For green space, Monterey Park has 10 parks, led by Garvey Ranch Park at roughly 19 acres. Golfers have Monterey Park Golf Club. For a day out there is Weingart Stadium, Vincent Price Art Museum and AMC Atlantic Times Square 14.

East Los Angeles College is here too. The map shows 83 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Japanese and Seafood the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Alhambra Unified, Garvey Elementary and Montebello Unified. On price, Monterey Park is the 61st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $910K for the typical home, and values are up 79% in ten years.

Monterey Park Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Monterey Park, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Monterey Park is worth about $909,564, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Monterey Park going up or down?

The typical home value in Monterey Park is up 1.5% over the past year, from about $896,510 to about $909,564. Over ten years it is up 79%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Monterey Park?

Typical values in Monterey Park by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $482,341, 2 bedroom about $739,991, 3 bedroom about $889,389, 4 bedroom about $1,064,263, 5+ bedroom about $1,317,673. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Monterey Park?

The typical asking rent in Monterey Park is about $2,638 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Monterey Park?

9 public schools sit inside the Monterey Park boundary: 8 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to Alhambra Unified, Garvey Elementary and Montebello Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Monterey Park?

Monterey Park has 10 parks. The largest are Garvey Ranch Park (about 19 acres), George E Elder Park (about 11 acres) and Barnes Memorial Park (about 10 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Monterey Park?

Monterey Park is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are East Los Angeles, Alhambra, Montebello and Rosemead.
