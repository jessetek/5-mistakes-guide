The Story of
Aliso Viejo.

Aliso Viejo is a city of about 50,457 people in Orange County.

Aliso Canyon Community Park, about 84 acres, is the largest of 17 parks here. Golfers have Aliso Viejo Resort and Golf Course. Local draws include Edwards Aliso Viejo Stadium 20 & IMAX.

Soka University of America is here too. The map shows 31 places to eat and drink, with Thai, Japanese and Ice cream the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Capistrano Unified. On price, Aliso Viejo is the 50th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $992K for the typical home, and values are up 89% in ten years.

Answered.

What is the typical home value in Aliso Viejo, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Aliso Viejo is worth about $992,406, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Aliso Viejo going up or down?

The typical home value in Aliso Viejo is up 2.1% over the past year, from about $971,692 to about $992,406. Over ten years it is up 89%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Aliso Viejo?

Typical values in Aliso Viejo by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $493,071, 2 bedroom about $744,992, 3 bedroom about $1,019,850, 4 bedroom about $1,497,008, 5+ bedroom about $2,083,855. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Aliso Viejo?

The typical asking rent in Aliso Viejo is about $3,484 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Aliso Viejo?

9 public schools sit inside the Aliso Viejo boundary: 5 elementary, 2 middle, 1 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Capistrano Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Aliso Viejo?

Aliso Viejo has 17 parks. The largest are Aliso Canyon Community Park (about 84 acres), Hillview Park (about 26 acres) and Ridgecrest Park (about 20 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Aliso Viejo?

Aliso Viejo is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Laguna Hills, Laguna Woods, Laguna Beach and Laguna Niguel.
