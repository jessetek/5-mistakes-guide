The Story of
Huntington Park.

About 52,166 people call Huntington Park home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

Its official start date is 1906, about 120 years ago. For green space, Huntington Park has 4 parks, led by Salt Lake Park at roughly 32 acres. For a day out there is Park Theatres.

The map shows 13 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Coffee and American the most common. There are 22 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Huntington Park is the 10th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $669K for the typical home, and values are up 95% in ten years.

Huntington Park Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Huntington Park, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Huntington Park is worth about $668,582, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Huntington Park going up or down?

The typical home value in Huntington Park is up 0.6% over the past year, from about $664,456 to about $668,582. Over ten years it is up 95%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Huntington Park?

Typical values in Huntington Park by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $515,524, 2 bedroom about $615,641, 3 bedroom about $689,113, 4 bedroom about $748,698, 5+ bedroom about $830,200. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Huntington Park?

The typical asking rent in Huntington Park is about $1,768 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Huntington Park?

22 public schools sit inside the Huntington Park boundary: 12 elementary, 3 middle, 5 high and 2 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Huntington Park?

Huntington Park has 4 parks. The largest are Salt Lake Park (about 32 acres), Raul R. Perez Memorial Park (about 4.7 acres) and Huntington Park City Park (about 3.3 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Huntington Park?

Huntington Park is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Walnut Park, Vernon, Florence-Graham and Maywood.
