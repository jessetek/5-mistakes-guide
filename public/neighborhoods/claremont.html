The Story of
Claremont.

About 36,139 people call Claremont home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from Claremont. For green space, Claremont has 25 parks, led by California Botanic Garden at roughly 85 acres. Open space and trails include Claremont Hills Wilderness Park and Sycamore Canyon Park. Golfers have Claremont Golf Course.

For a day out there is Laemmle's Claremont 5, Raymond M. Alf Museum of Paleontology and Benton Museum of Art. Claremont McKenna College is here too. The map shows 57 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Italian and Mexican the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Claremont Unified. On price, Claremont is the 46th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.01M for the typical home, and values are up 75% in ten years.

Claremont Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Claremont, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Claremont is worth about $1,010,852, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Claremont going up or down?

The typical home value in Claremont is up 2.2% over the past year, from about $989,387 to about $1,010,852. Over ten years it is up 75%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Claremont?

Typical values in Claremont by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $700,737, 3 bedroom about $895,204, 4 bedroom about $1,115,513, 5+ bedroom about $1,663,102. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Claremont?

The typical asking rent in Claremont is about $3,128 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Claremont?

9 public schools sit inside the Claremont boundary: 7 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Claremont Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Claremont?

Claremont has 25 parks. The largest are California Botanic Garden (about 85 acres), Padua Avenue Park (about 26 acres) and Cahuilla Park (about 18 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Claremont?

Claremont is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Upland, La Verne, Montclair and Pomona.
