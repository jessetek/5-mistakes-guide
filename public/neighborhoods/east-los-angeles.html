The Story of
East Los Angeles.

East Los Angeles is a unincorporated community of about 118,786 people in Los Angeles County.

Belvedere Park, about 44 acres, is the largest of 6 parks here. Local draws include Coyolxauhqui Plaza.

The map shows 32 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and Seafood the most common. There are 36 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified and Montebello Unified. On price, East Los Angeles is the 12th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $671K for the typical home, and values are up 90% in ten years.

East Los Angeles Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in East Los Angeles, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in East Los Angeles is worth about $671,284, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in East Los Angeles going up or down?

The typical home value in East Los Angeles is up 0.1% over the past year, from about $670,331 to about $671,284. Over ten years it is up 90%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in East Los Angeles?

Typical values in East Los Angeles by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $519,303, 2 bedroom about $624,596, 3 bedroom about $699,022, 4 bedroom about $754,406, 5+ bedroom about $818,872. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in East Los Angeles?

The typical asking rent in East Los Angeles is about $2,125 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in East Los Angeles?

36 public schools sit inside the East Los Angeles boundary: 22 elementary, 6 middle and 8 high. They belong to Los Angeles Unified and Montebello Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in East Los Angeles?

East Los Angeles has 6 parks. The largest are Belvedere Park (about 44 acres), City Terrace Park (about 15 acres) and Obregon Park (about 11 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near East Los Angeles?

East Los Angeles is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Monterey Park, Commerce, Maywood and Vernon.
