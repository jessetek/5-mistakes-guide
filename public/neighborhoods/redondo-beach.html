The Story of
Redondo Beach.

Redondo Beach, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 68,075 residents.

For green space, Redondo Beach has 14 parks, led by Dominguez Park at roughly 20 acres. It has ocean beach: Redondo Beach State Park.

For a day out there is AMC South Bay Galleria 16. The map shows 169 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Mexican and Seafood the most common. There are 11 public schools inside the boundary, run by Redondo Beach Unified.

Why Redondo Beach

What Makes Redondo Beach
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🌊

True Coastal Without 7-Figure Premium

Manhattan Beach starts at $3M+. Redondo gets you walkable-to-pier homes at $1.5M-$2.5M. The sweet spot.

🏫

Strong Beach Schools

Redondo Unified is well-rated. RBHS feeds top UCs. South Redondo elementaries are particularly strong.

🚚

Walkable Riviera Village + Pier

Two distinct walkable downtowns. RV in South Redondo, the Pier district in North. Coffee shops, restaurants, real life on foot.

🏖️

Beach Cities Lifestyle

Bike path the entire 22-mile Strand. Sunset volleyball, pier farmers market, Riviera 4th of July run. South Bay culture is real here.

Where to Live
in Redondo Beach.

A real-talk breakdown of the Redondo Beach sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

South Redondo / Hollywood Riviera

Premium hilltop pocket. Ocean views, top schools, mid-luxury Redondo.

📍

North Redondo / Golden Hills

Inland family neighborhoods. Good elementaries, larger lots, sub-$1.5M entry.

📍

Riviera Village

Walkable South Redondo restaurant/shop district. Townhomes + condos plus restored cottages.

📍

North Redondo / Aviation Corridor

Mid-price townhomes and SFRs. Aviation HS feeder. Solid value entry to RB.
