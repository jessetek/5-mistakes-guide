The Story of
Montclair.

Montclair, in San Bernardino County, is a city of roughly 37,650 residents.

It dates to 1956. There are 10 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Saratoga Park, at about 12 acres. For a day out there is Montclair Place.

The map shows 34 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Boba tea and Mexican the most common. There are 11 public schools inside the boundary, run by Ontario-Montclair. On price, Montclair is the 7th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $653K for the typical home, and values are up 88% in ten years.

Montclair Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Montclair, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Montclair is worth about $653,332, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Montclair going up or down?

The typical home value in Montclair is up 0.6% over the past year, from about $649,697 to about $653,332. Over ten years it is up 88%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Montclair?

Typical values in Montclair by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $520,138, 3 bedroom about $651,313, 4 bedroom about $685,152, 5+ bedroom about $790,311. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Montclair?

The typical asking rent in Montclair is about $2,895 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Montclair?

11 public schools sit inside the Montclair boundary: 8 elementary, 2 middle and 1 high. They belong to Ontario-Montclair. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Montclair?

Montclair has 10 parks. The largest are Saratoga Park (about 12 acres), Sunset Park (about 8.1 acres) and Wilderness Basin Park (about 5.5 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Montclair?

Montclair is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Pomona, Upland, Claremont and La Verne.
