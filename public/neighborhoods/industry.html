The Story of
Industry.

About 256 people call Industry home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from secondary sector of the economy. Industry Hills Recreation Center is the main park here. Golfers have The Industry Hills Golf Club - Babe Zaharias Course.

Local draws include Workman and Temple Family Homestead Museum, AMC Puente Hills 20 and Vineland Drive-in. The map shows 32 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, American and Boba tea the most common. There are 2 public schools inside the boundary, run by Bassett Unified.
