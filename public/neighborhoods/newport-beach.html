The place

The Story of
Newport Beach.

Newport Beach, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 82,970 residents.

It has been its own community since 1870. Upper Newport Bay Nature Preserve, about 168 acres, is the largest of 33 parks here. It has ocean beach: Newport Dunes, North Star Beach and Edgewater. Open space and trails include Buck Gully Reserve and Environmental Nature Center (ENC). Golfers have Newport Beach Country Club and Newport Beach Golf Course.

For a day out there is Edwards Big Newport 6, Balboa Fun Zone and Fashion Island. Orange Coast College Waterfront Campus is here too. The map shows 196 places to eat and drink, with American, Coffee and Seafood the most common. There are 12 public schools inside the boundary, run by Newport-Mesa Unified. On price, Newport Beach is the 1st highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $3.71M for the typical home, and values are up 130% in ten years.

Why Newport Beach

What Makes Newport Beach
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

⛵

Yacht Harbor Lifestyle

Newport Harbor is the largest small-craft harbor on the West Coast. Boating culture is real here, and waterfront homes are generational assets.

🏝️

Iconic Sub-Markets

Balboa Island, Corona del Mar, Newport Coast, Eastbluff, each is its own market with its own ceiling. Knowing which is which is the whole game.

🏫

Newport-Mesa USD's Best

Corona del Mar High and Newport Harbor High are well-regarded. Buyers in CdM zones pay a real premium for the school feeders.

💎

Liquidity at the Top

Newport's $5M+ market is one of the most active in California, international buyers, tech wealth, second homes. Off-market deals are common.

Where to Live
in Newport Beach.

A real-talk breakdown of the Newport Beach sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

Corona del Mar (CdM)

Pacific Coast Highway charm, walkable village, top schools. Mid-luxury sweet spot at $3M-$8M.

📍

Balboa Island

Iconic small-island living, ferry to the peninsula. Tight-knit community, cottage-scale lots.

📍

Newport Coast / Pelican

Custom hilltop estates, ocean views, gated. The $10M+ ceiling lives here.

📍

Eastbluff / Bluffs

Family-friendly inland Newport. Strong schools, more reasonable mid-luxury entry point.
