The Story of
Signal Hill.

About 11,279 people call Signal Hill home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1924. For green space, Signal Hill has 6 parks, led by Signal Hill Park at roughly 8.7 acres. American University of Health Sciences is here too.

The map shows 9 places to eat and drink. There are 4 public schools inside the boundary, run by Long Beach Unified. On price, Signal Hill is the 21st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $700K for the typical home, and values are up 60% in ten years.

Signal Hill Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Signal Hill, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Signal Hill is worth about $700,178, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Signal Hill going up or down?

The typical home value in Signal Hill is up 0.1% over the past year, from about $699,821 to about $700,178. Over ten years it is up 60%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Signal Hill?

Typical values in Signal Hill by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $427,097, 2 bedroom about $585,103, 3 bedroom about $870,953, 4 bedroom about $1,295,108. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Signal Hill?

The typical asking rent in Signal Hill is about $2,586 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Signal Hill?

4 public schools sit inside the Signal Hill boundary: 2 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Long Beach Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Signal Hill?

Signal Hill has 6 parks. The largest are Signal Hill Park (about 8.7 acres), Discovery Well Park (about 4.5 acres) and Reservoir Park (about 3.7 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Signal Hill?

Signal Hill is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Long Beach, Lakewood, Carson and Hawaiian Gardens.
