The place

The Story of
Culver City.

About 39,883 people call Culver City home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from Harry Culver. For green space, Culver City has 14 parks, led by Veterans Memorial Park at roughly 11 acres. Open space and trails include Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook.

Places people go: Westfield Culver City, Kirk Douglas Theatre and The Culver Theater. The map shows 189 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Pizza and Mexican the most common. There are 8 public schools inside the boundary, run by Culver City Unified.

Why Culver City

What Makes Culver City
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🚶

Truly Walkable Downtown

Downtown Culver City is one of LA's best walkable cores, restaurants, theaters, parks, transit. Real urbanism in the right scale.

🏫

CCUSD Schools: Strong Westside Public

Culver City Unified is well-rated. CCHS, MS, and elementaries all reasonably strong. Westside families pick CC over private when the math works.

🎬

Studios + Tech Adjacency

Sony, Amazon Studios, Apple, HBO. Culver City is the Westside's media production hub. Stable employment ecosystem.

🚚

Expo Line Access

Direct light rail to Downtown LA, Santa Monica, USC. Rare LA city where transit actually works for daily commuting.
