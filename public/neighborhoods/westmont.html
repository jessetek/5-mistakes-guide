Answered.

What is the typical home value in Westmont, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Westmont is worth about $691,910, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Westmont going up or down?

The typical home value in Westmont is up 0.7% over the past year, from about $687,192 to about $691,910. Over ten years it is up 110%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Westmont?

Typical values in Westmont by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $516,606, 2 bedroom about $600,213, 3 bedroom about $702,086, 4 bedroom about $772,237, 5+ bedroom about $844,032. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Westmont?

The typical asking rent in Westmont is about $2,333 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Westmont?

6 public schools sit inside the Westmont boundary: 2 elementary, 3 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What cities are near Westmont?

Westmont is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Inglewood, West Rancho Dominguez, Hawthorne and Lennox.
