The place

The Story of
Westminster.

Westminster, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 89,537 residents.

It has been its own community since 1870. For green space, Westminster has 18 parks, led by Elden F Gillespie Park at roughly 13 acres. Local draws include Phước Lộc Thọ;Asian Garden Mall, Westminster Mall and Regency Theatres.

The map shows 59 places to eat and drink, with Vietnamese, Coffee and Pizza the most common. There are 20 public schools inside the boundary, run by Westminster, Garden Grove Unified and Ocean View. On price, Westminster is the 40th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.09M for the typical home, and values are up 98% in ten years.

Why Westminster

What Makes Westminster
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

💵

Coastal-OC Pricing Without Coastal Premium

A $1.19M median sale price, below Huntington Beach ($1.42M) just 10 min west (Redfin, three months ending October 2026). Same general location, different price point.

🌏

Strong Asian-American Community

Asian Garden Mall, Vietnamese-American businesses along Bolsa, Korean enclaves. Cultural depth most OC cities lack.

🛣️

405 + 22 Access

Direct 405 access to Long Beach (15 min), LAX (35 min). 22 takes you to Anaheim and the I-5 corridor in 10.

🏖️

20 Min to Beaches

Huntington and Bolsa Chica beaches are 15-20 min west. Real coastal-OC quality of life without coastal-OC pricing.
