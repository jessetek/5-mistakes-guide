The Story of
Cypress.

Cypress is a city of about 49,286 people in Orange County.

Oak Knoll Park, about 21 acres, is the largest of 20 parks here. Local draws include Forest Lawn Memorial Park - Cypress. Cypress College is here too.

The map shows 44 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Boba tea and Asian the most common. There are 10 public schools inside the boundary, run by Cypress Elementary and Anaheim Union High. On price, Cypress is the 44th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.07M for the typical home, and values are up 92% in ten years.

Why Cypress

What Makes Cypress
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🎓

Top OC Schools

Cypress feeds into Cypress High and Oxford Academy. Oxford is one of California's top-ranked public schools. School quality directly shapes Cypress home values.

🛡️

Low Crime, Walkable Neighborhoods

Cypress consistently ranks in the top quintile for OC safety. Master-planned in the 60s, so streets are quiet and lots are generous.

📍

Central OC Location

405, 22, 91 freeways converge nearby. Beach cities are 20 min south, Disneyland is 15 min east, downtown LA is 35 min northwest.

📈

Solid Long-Term Appreciation

Cypress home values have outperformed broader OC by ~1-2% annually over the last decade. Quiet wealth-builder market.
