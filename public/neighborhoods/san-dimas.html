The Story of
San Dimas.

About 33,226 people call San Dimas home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1960. Frank G. Bonelli Regional Park, about 1,787 acres, is the largest of 12 parks here. Golfers have San Dimas Canyon Golf Course and Via Verde Country Club. Places people go: Raging Waters, Pacific Railroad Museum and Bonita Center for the Arts.

Life Pacific University is here too. The map shows 19 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and American the most common. There are 7 public schools inside the boundary, run by Bonita Unified. On price, San Dimas is the 59th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $902K for the typical home, and values are up 77% in ten years.

What is the typical home value in San Dimas, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in San Dimas is worth about $902,110, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in San Dimas going up or down?

The typical home value in San Dimas is about the same as a year ago, near $902,110. Over ten years it is up 77%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in San Dimas?

Typical values in San Dimas by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $617,720, 3 bedroom about $835,071, 4 bedroom about $1,023,346, 5+ bedroom about $1,288,333. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in San Dimas?

The typical asking rent in San Dimas is about $2,761 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in San Dimas?

7 public schools sit inside the San Dimas boundary: 5 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Bonita Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in San Dimas?

San Dimas has 12 parks. The largest are Frank G. Bonelli Regional Park (about 1,787 acres), Horsethief Canyon Park (about 108 acres) and San Dimas Canyon Park (about 52 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near San Dimas?

San Dimas is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are La Verne, Glendora, Pomona and Covina.
