The Story of
Rancho Cucamonga.

Rancho Cucamonga, in San Bernardino County, is a city of roughly 176,675 residents.

Rancho Cucamonga Central Park, about 105 acres, is the largest of 34 parks here. Open space and trails include North Etiwanda Preserve. Golfers have Red Hill Country Club. Local draws include Terra Vista 6 Cinemas, AMC Victoria Gardens and Victoria Gardens.

Chaffey College is here too. The map shows 117 places to eat and drink, with American, Mexican and Pizza the most common. There are 36 public schools inside the boundary, run by Etiwanda Elementary, Alta Loma Elementary and Central Elementary and others. On price, Rancho Cucamonga is the 37th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $779K for the typical home, and values are up 72% in ten years.

Rancho Cucamonga Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Rancho Cucamonga, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Rancho Cucamonga is worth about $779,249, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Rancho Cucamonga going up or down?

The typical home value in Rancho Cucamonga is up 0.2% over the past year, from about $777,741 to about $779,249. Over ten years it is up 72%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Rancho Cucamonga?

Typical values in Rancho Cucamonga by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $335,274, 2 bedroom about $487,630, 3 bedroom about $703,325, 4 bedroom about $884,505, 5+ bedroom about $1,165,380. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Rancho Cucamonga?

The typical asking rent in Rancho Cucamonga is about $2,786 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Rancho Cucamonga?

36 public schools sit inside the Rancho Cucamonga boundary: 23 elementary, 8 middle, 4 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Etiwanda Elementary, Alta Loma Elementary, Central Elementary, Chaffey Joint Union High and Cucamonga Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Rancho Cucamonga?

Rancho Cucamonga has 34 parks. The largest are Rancho Cucamonga Central Park (about 105 acres), Red Hill Community Park (about 44 acres) and Heritage Community Park (about 41 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Rancho Cucamonga?

Rancho Cucamonga is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Upland, Ontario, Claremont and Montclair.
