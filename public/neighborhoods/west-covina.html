The place

The Story of
West Covina.

West Covina, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 106,920 residents.

Galster Wilderness Park, about 58 acres, is the largest of 16 parks here. Golfers have South Hills Country Club. Places people go: Edwards West Covina Stadium 18 and Plaza West Covina.

The map shows 56 places to eat and drink, with American, Filipino and Coffee the most common. There are 23 public schools inside the boundary, run by West Covina Unified, Covina-Valley Unified and Rowland Unified. On price, West Covina is the 47th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $835K for the typical home, and values are up 84% in ten years.

Why West Covina

What Makes West Covina
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🌐

Real Neighborhood Variety

South Hills luxury, central walkable, North Vincent value. West Covina gives buyers a wider price spread than most SGV cities.

🛣️

10 + 605 Freeway Access

Direct 10 freeway access, downtown LA in 25 min, IE in 25. 605 connects to Long Beach in 35 min.

🛍️

Westfield West Covina Mall

One of the East SGV's main commercial anchors. Shopping, dining, and walkability infrastructure most LA suburbs lack.

🏫

Multiple School Districts

West Covina USD, Walnut Valley USD, Rowland USD all serve different parts of the city. Buyers shop districts as carefully as homes.
