The Story of
Carson.

About 91,358 people call Carson home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1968. There are 15 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Victoria Community Regional Park, at about 35 acres. Open space and trails include Dominguez Nature Preserve. Golfers have Victoria Golf Course.

For a day out there is Dignity Health Tennis Center and Cinemark Carson and XD. California State University - Dominguez Hills is here too. The map shows 53 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Filipino and Japanese the most common. There are 22 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Carson is the 41st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $787K for the typical home, and values are up 93% in ten years.

Carson Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Carson, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Carson is worth about $787,368, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Carson going up or down?

The typical home value in Carson is up 1.1% over the past year, from about $778,618 to about $787,368. Over ten years it is up 93%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Carson?

Typical values in Carson by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $404,094, 2 bedroom about $591,642, 3 bedroom about $762,264, 4 bedroom about $841,209, 5+ bedroom about $901,480. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Carson?

The typical asking rent in Carson is about $2,909 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Carson?

22 public schools sit inside the Carson boundary: 14 elementary, 3 middle, 4 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Carson?

Carson has 15 parks. The largest are Victoria Community Regional Park (about 35 acres), Vernon Hemingway Memorial Park (about 16 acres) and Dolphin Park (about 13 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Carson?

Carson is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are West Carson, Compton, West Rancho Dominguez and East Rancho Dominguez.
