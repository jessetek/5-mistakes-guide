The Story of
Covina.

Covina is a city of about 49,245 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1882. For green space, Covina has 12 parks, led by Wilderness Park at roughly 17 acres. For a day out there is AMC Covina 17.

Charter Oak Adult Education is here too. The map shows 32 places to eat and drink, with American, Mexican and Coffee the most common. There are 11 public schools inside the boundary, run by Covina-Valley Unified and Charter Oak Unified. On price, Covina is the 42nd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $791K for the typical home, and values are up 83% in ten years.

Covina Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Covina, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Covina is worth about $791,019, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Covina going up or down?

The typical home value in Covina is up 0.8% over the past year, from about $784,836 to about $791,019. Over ten years it is up 83%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Covina?

Typical values in Covina by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $485,134, 2 bedroom about $597,328, 3 bedroom about $779,625, 4 bedroom about $848,447, 5+ bedroom about $1,067,080. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Covina?

The typical asking rent in Covina is about $2,598 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Covina?

11 public schools sit inside the Covina boundary: 6 elementary, 2 middle and 3 high. They belong to Covina-Valley Unified and Charter Oak Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Covina?

Covina has 12 parks. The largest are Wilderness Park (about 17 acres), Kahler Russell Park (about 16 acres) and Covina Park (about 11 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Covina?

Covina is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Vincent, West Covina, Azusa and Walnut.
