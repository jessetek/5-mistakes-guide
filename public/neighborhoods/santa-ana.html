The place

The Story of
Santa Ana.

Santa Ana is a city of about 316,184 people in Orange County.

It dates to 1869. The name comes from Saint Anne. There are 36 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Centennial Park, at about 85 acres. Golfers have Riverview Golf Course and Willowick Municipal Golf Course.

Places people go: The Frida Cinema, Yost Theater and Discovery Science Center. Santa Ana College is here too. The map shows 137 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and American the most common. There are 70 public schools inside the boundary, run by Santa Ana Unified and Garden Grove Unified. On price, Santa Ana is the 50th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $859K for the typical home, and values are up 99% in ten years.

Why Santa Ana

What Makes Santa Ana
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏛️

Historic Architecture

Floral Park, Park Santiago, French Park. Santa Ana has the best concentration of 1920s-30s historic homes in OC. Bungalows, Tudors, Spanish Colonials.

💰

Best Value in OC

Median sale price around $829K (Redfin, three months ending October 2026), well below most of OC. Largest delta-per-square-foot in OC. Money goes further here.

🌮

Real Cultural Hub

Diverse neighborhoods, authentic food scene, OC's actual urban downtown. Real community, not a planned community.

🛣️

Central OC Access

5, 22, 55, 57 freeways all converge in Santa Ana. Best commuting flexibility in OC.
