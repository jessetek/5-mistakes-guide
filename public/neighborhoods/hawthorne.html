The Story of
Hawthorne.

Hawthorne, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 83,633 residents.

It has been its own community since 1922. The name comes from Nathaniel Hawthorne. For green space, Hawthorne has 9 parks, led by Holly Park at roughly 13 acres.

For a day out there is Falcon 9 Booster B1019. The map shows 25 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Chinese and Mexican the most common. There are 17 public schools inside the boundary, run by Hawthorne and Wiseburn Unified. On price, Hawthorne is the 51st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $863K for the typical home, and values are up 80% in ten years.

Hawthorne Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Hawthorne, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Hawthorne is worth about $863,012, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Hawthorne going up or down?

The typical home value in Hawthorne is up 1.6% over the past year, from about $849,791 to about $863,012. Over ten years it is up 80%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Hawthorne?

Typical values in Hawthorne by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $634,886, 2 bedroom about $764,773, 3 bedroom about $861,953, 4 bedroom about $991,619, 5+ bedroom about $1,152,367. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Hawthorne?

The typical asking rent in Hawthorne is about $2,151 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Hawthorne?

17 public schools sit inside the Hawthorne boundary: 10 elementary, 4 middle and 3 high. They belong to Hawthorne and Wiseburn Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Hawthorne?

Hawthorne has 9 parks. The largest are Holly Park (about 13 acres), Jim Thorpe Park (about 8.5 acres) and Glasgow Park (about 5.8 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Hawthorne?

Hawthorne is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Lennox, Lawndale, Gardena and Inglewood.
