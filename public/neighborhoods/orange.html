The place

The Story of
Orange.

About 137,941 people call Orange home. It sits in Orange County.

Its official start date is 1869, about 157 years ago. For green space, Orange has 24 parks, led by El Modena Open Space at roughly 147 acres. Open space and trails include Cerro Villa Park. Golfers have Super Range Golf.

For a day out there is AMC Orange 30, Century Stadium 25 and XD and The Outlets at Orange. Chapman University is here too. The map shows 188 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, American and Coffee the most common. There are 25 public schools inside the boundary, run by Orange Unified. On price, Orange is the 36th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.12M for the typical home, and values are up 91% in ten years.

Why Orange

What Makes Orange
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏛️

Old Towne Orange Historic Plaza

National Register Historic District, the most authentic walkable downtown in OC. Independent restaurants, antique row, the Plaza fountain.

🎓

Chapman University + Strong K-12

Chapman drives a steady educated population. Orange Unified has solid feeders, especially East Orange schools.

🏘️

Architectural Variety

Old Towne has 1880s Victorians and Craftsman bungalows. East Orange has 1960s ranch homes. North Orange has new construction. Real range.

🛣️

Multi-Freeway Access

5, 22, 55, 57, 91 all serve Orange. Most freeway-flexible city in central OC.
