The Story of
Corona.

Corona is a city of about 161,540 people in Riverside County.

For green space, Corona has 45 parks, led by Santana Regional Park at roughly 47 acres. Golfers have Eagle Glen Golf Club and Dos Lagos Golf Course. Local draws include Starlight Dos Lagos 15 and Edwards Stadium 18 Theatre.

HIS University is here too. The map shows 82 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, American and Coffee the most common. There are 26 public schools inside the boundary, run by Corona-Norco Unified. On price, Corona is the 30th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $751K for the typical home, and values are up 77% in ten years.

Corona Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Corona, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Corona is worth about $750,756, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Corona going up or down?

The typical home value in Corona is up 0.2% over the past year, from about $749,047 to about $750,756. Over ten years it is up 77%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Corona?

Typical values in Corona by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $335,979, 2 bedroom about $534,163, 3 bedroom about $684,923, 4 bedroom about $805,876, 5+ bedroom about $998,849. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Corona?

The typical asking rent in Corona is about $2,661 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Corona?

26 public schools sit inside the Corona boundary: 17 elementary, 4 middle, 4 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Corona-Norco Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Corona?

Corona has 45 parks. The largest are Santana Regional Park (about 47 acres), Butterfield Park (about 44 acres) and Mountain Gate Park (about 21 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Corona?

Corona is in Riverside County. The closest cities I cover are Norco, Eastvale, Temescal Valley and Chino.
