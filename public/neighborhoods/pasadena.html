The place

The Story of
Pasadena.

Pasadena is a city of about 137,195 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1886. There are 28 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Brookside Park, at about 32 acres. Open space and trails include Hahamongna Watershed Natural Park and Upper Arroyo Seco. Golfers have Annandale Country Club and Eaton Canyon Golf Course.

Places people go: Rose Bowl Stadium, Kidspace Children's Museum and USC Pacific Asia Museum. Pasadena City College is here too. The map shows 286 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, American and Mexican the most common. There are 21 public schools inside the boundary, run by Pasadena Unified. On price, Pasadena is the 28th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.19M for the typical home, and values are up 79% in ten years.

Why Pasadena

What Makes Pasadena
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏛️

Walkable Old Town + Real Downtown

Few LA cities have this kind of historic, walkable downtown, restaurants, theaters, galleries, the Pasadena Playhouse, and a true urban experience.

🎓

Pasadena Unified + Private School Density

PUSD has strong feeders, and Pasadena has one of LA's highest concentrations of top-tier private schools (Polytechnic, Mayfield, Westridge).

🌹

Cultural Capital

Norton Simon Museum, the Huntington, the Rose Bowl, Caltech adjacent. The cultural depth of a much bigger city in a residential package.

🏠

Architectural Crown Jewel of LA

More original Craftsman homes than any other US city. Spanish Revival, Tudor, mid-century modern, and contemporary all coexist here.
