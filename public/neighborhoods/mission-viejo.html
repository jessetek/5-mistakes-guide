The place

The Story of
Mission Viejo.

Mission Viejo, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 91,601 residents.

For green space, Mission Viejo has 46 parks, led by Oso Viejo Community Park at roughly 91 acres. Golfers have Mission Viejo Golf Club and Oso Creek Golf Course. For a day out there is Regal Edwards Kaleidoscope and The Shops at Mission Viejo.

Saddleback College is here too. The map shows 67 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Mexican and Italian the most common. There are 19 public schools inside the boundary, run by Saddleback Valley Unified and Capistrano Unified. On price, Mission Viejo is the 27th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.2M for the typical home, and values are up 96% in ten years.

Lake Mission Viejo

Private 124-acre lake exclusive to Mission Viejo residents. Beach club, sailing, swim lessons, a real amenity, not a marketing line.
