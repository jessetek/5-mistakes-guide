The Story of
North Tustin.

About 25,718 people call North Tustin home. It sits in Orange County.

Esplanade Park is the main park here. Open space and trails include Peters Canyon Regional Park.

There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Tustin Unified. On price, North Tustin is the 10th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.75M for the typical home, and values are up 109% in ten years.

Answered.

What is the typical home value in North Tustin, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in North Tustin is worth about $1,751,878, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in North Tustin going up or down?

The typical home value in North Tustin is up 4.0% over the past year, from about $1,684,166 to about $1,751,878. Over ten years it is up 109%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in North Tustin?

Typical values in North Tustin by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $1,124,863, 3 bedroom about $1,447,652, 4 bedroom about $1,825,068, 5+ bedroom about $2,379,793. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in North Tustin?

The typical asking rent in North Tustin is about $5,100 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in North Tustin?

9 public schools sit inside the North Tustin boundary: 7 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Tustin Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What cities are near North Tustin?

North Tustin is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Tustin, Villa Park, Orange and Santa Ana.
