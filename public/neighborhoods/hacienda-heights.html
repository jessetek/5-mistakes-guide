The Story of
Hacienda Heights.

About 54,191 people call Hacienda Heights home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

There are 9 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Peter F Schabarum Regional County Park, at about 573 acres. Open space and trails include Puente Hills Preserve. Local draws include Dave Merrill Stadium and Little Theater.

The map shows 12 places to eat and drink. There are 12 public schools inside the boundary, run by Hacienda la Puente Unified. On price, Hacienda Heights is the 55th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $942K for the typical home, and values are up 89% in ten years.

Why Hacienda Heights

What Makes Hacienda Heights
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🥾

Hiking + Hillside Character

Schabarum Trail, Hsi Lai Temple grounds, and the Puente Hills behind the city give Hacienda Heights real outdoor access uncommon for LA County suburbs.

🎓

Hacienda La Puente USD

HLPUSD has several solid elementary feeders. Buyers shop specific catchment areas.

🏘️

Real Single-Family Character

Mostly SFR, larger lots than the LA basin average, mature landscaping. Different feel than master-planned tracts.

🛣️

60 Freeway + 605 Access

Direct 60 freeway access to downtown LA (30 min) and Inland Empire (20 min). 605 takes you to Whittier and Long Beach in 25.
