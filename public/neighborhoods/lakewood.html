The place

The Story of
Lakewood.

Lakewood is a city of about 78,494 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1954. Rynerson Park, about 34 acres, is the largest of 14 parks here. Golfers have Lakewood Country Club.

Local draws include Lakewood Center Mall. The map shows 70 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Coffee and Ice cream the most common. There are 17 public schools inside the boundary, run by Long Beach Unified, Bellflower Unified and ABC Unified. On price, Lakewood is the 53rd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $872K for the typical home, and values are up 83% in ten years.

Why Lakewood

What Makes Lakewood
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏡

America's Original Master-Planned Suburb

Built in 1950 as the largest planned community in U.S. history. The street grids, parks, and shopping centers still feel intentional 75 years later.

🎓

Long Beach + ABC School Choice

Lakewood spans LBUSD and ABC Unified, two top SoCal districts. Buyers can target specific elementary feeders for value.

🛍️

Lakewood Center Mall

One of LA's first regional malls, recently revitalized. Anchor for the city's commercial identity and a real walkability win.

🚀

Stable Demand, Tight Inventory

Lakewood is a multi-generation hold market, homes pass between families. Inventory is consistently tight, which keeps values strong.
