The Story of
Ontario.

Ontario, in San Bernardino County, is a city of roughly 185,285 residents.

The name comes from Ontario. For green space, Ontario has 34 parks, led by Ontario Grand Park at roughly 137 acres. Golfers have Whispering Lakes Golf Course. Local draws include Ontario Mills, Chaffey Community Museum of Art and AMC DINE-IN Ontario Mills 30.

Everest College is here too. The map shows 92 places to eat and drink, with American, Mexican and Pizza the most common. There are 34 public schools inside the boundary, run by Ontario-Montclair, Mountain View Elementary and Chaffey Joint Union High and others. On price, Ontario is the 9th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $662K for the typical home, and values are up 88% in ten years.

Ontario Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Ontario, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Ontario is worth about $662,006, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Ontario going up or down?

The typical home value in Ontario is down 0.1% over the past year, from about $662,515 to about $662,006. Over ten years it is up 88%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Ontario?

Typical values in Ontario by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $397,606, 2 bedroom about $509,081, 3 bedroom about $643,260, 4 bedroom about $739,601, 5+ bedroom about $872,006. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Ontario?

The typical asking rent in Ontario is about $2,580 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Ontario?

34 public schools sit inside the Ontario boundary: 25 elementary, 6 middle and 3 high. They belong to Ontario-Montclair, Mountain View Elementary, Chaffey Joint Union High and Chino Valley Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Ontario?

Ontario has 34 parks. The largest are Ontario Grand Park (about 137 acres), Cucamonga-Guasti Regional Park (about 67 acres) and John Galvin Park (about 34 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Ontario?

Ontario is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Eastvale, Chino, Montclair and Upland.
