The Story of
Rosemead.

Rosemead, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 49,582 residents.

For green space, Rosemead has 8 parks, led by Rosemead Park at roughly 27 acres. Places people go: Rod Marinelli Stadium. PCC at Rosemead is here too.

The map shows 52 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Vietnamese and Seafood the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by Garvey Elementary and Rosemead Elementary. On price, Rosemead is the 57th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $890K for the typical home, and values are up 81% in ten years.

Rosemead Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Rosemead, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Rosemead is worth about $889,995, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Rosemead going up or down?

The typical home value in Rosemead is up 0.8% over the past year, from about $882,738 to about $889,995. Over ten years it is up 81%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Rosemead?

Typical values in Rosemead by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $650,698, 2 bedroom about $771,119, 3 bedroom about $876,049, 4 bedroom about $1,046,040, 5+ bedroom about $1,204,828. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Rosemead?

The typical asking rent in Rosemead is about $2,755 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Rosemead?

14 public schools sit inside the Rosemead boundary: 10 elementary, 3 middle and 1 high. They belong to Garvey Elementary and Rosemead Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Rosemead?

Rosemead has 8 parks. The largest are Rosemead Park (about 27 acres), Garvey Park (about 13 acres) and Zapopan Park (about 6.8 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Rosemead?

Rosemead is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are San Gabriel, South El Monte, Temple City and El Monte.
