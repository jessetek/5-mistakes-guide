The Story of
Bell.

Bell, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 31,930 residents.

It has been its own community since 1913. Veterans Memorial Park, about 3.3 acres, is the largest of 4 parks here.

There are 8 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Bell is the 16th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $690K for the typical home, and values are up 100% in ten years.

Bell Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Bell, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Bell is worth about $689,657, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Bell going up or down?

The typical home value in Bell is up 2.7% over the past year, from about $671,679 to about $689,657. Over ten years it is up 100%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Bell?

Typical values in Bell by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $638,713, 3 bedroom about $715,159, 4 bedroom about $762,126. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Bell?

The typical asking rent in Bell is about $2,550 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Bell?

8 public schools sit inside the Bell boundary: 4 elementary, 3 middle and 1 high. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Bell?

Bell has 4 parks. The largest are Veterans Memorial Park (about 3.3 acres), Ernest Debs Park (about 2.1 acres) and Treder Park (about 1.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Bell?

Bell is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Maywood, Cudahy, Bell Gardens and Commerce.
