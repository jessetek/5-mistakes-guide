The place

The Story of
Rowland Heights.

Rowland Heights is a unincorporated community of about 48,231 people in Los Angeles County.

Trailview Park, about 56 acres, is the largest of 6 parks here. Places people go: Boy Scout Amphitheater and Yes Plaza.

The map shows 35 places to eat and drink, with Korean, Chinese and Boba tea the most common. There are 8 public schools inside the boundary, run by Rowland Unified. On price, Rowland Heights is the 53rd highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $972K for the typical home, and values are up 66% in ten years.

Why Rowland Heights

What Makes Rowland Heights
Worth Calling Home.

🥟

World-Class Food Scene

Colima Road has one of the densest concentrations of regional Chinese, Korean, Taiwanese, and Vietnamese restaurants outside of San Gabriel. Real culinary destination.

🏫

Rowland Unified School District

RUSD ranks well, with several elementary feeders ranked in the top 20% of LA County. Schools shape buyer demand patterns here.

🛣️

Inland-LA Border Location

On the 60 freeway between LA and Inland Empire. 30 min to downtown LA, 25 to OC, 20 to Inland Empire job centers.

📈

Steady Long-Term Appreciation

Rowland Heights values have outperformed broader LA over the last decade, community demand is consistently strong.
