The place

The Story of
Santa Monica.

Santa Monica is a city of about 90,729 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1769. The name comes from Monica of Hippo. There are 23 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Clover Park, at about 18 acres. It has ocean beach: Palisades Beach, Santa Monica State Beach and Bay Street Beach.

Places people go: Museum of Flying, AMC Broadway 4 and AMC Santa Monica 7. Emperors College of Traditional Oriental Medicine is here too. The map shows 305 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, American and Italian the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Santa Monica-Malibu Unified.

Why Santa Monica

What Makes Santa Monica
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏖️

Beach + Walkability

Beach Bike Path, Third Street Promenade, Main Street, Montana Ave, walkable retail in multiple neighborhoods. Most pedestrian-friendly LA city.

💼

Silicon Beach Hub

Snap, Hulu, Riot, and 100+ tech companies based in SM. Tech-money buyer pool keeps the market hot.

🏫

SMMUSD Schools

Santa Monica-Malibu USD has well-rated elementaries. Samohi (SM High) is solid for public LA. Buyers compare to Brentwood + Beverly Hills.

💄

North of Montana

One of LA's most expensive single neighborhoods. Lots of $5M-$15M trades. Drives the Santa Monica luxury comp.

Family neighborhood, sub-$2M entry available. Closer to airport but quiet streets.

Wilshire / Mid-City SM

Mid-priced condos and townhomes. Closer to retail and Westside corridors.

📍

Pico Blvd / Eastside SM

More attainable SM. Mix of condos and small SFRs, good elementary feeders.
