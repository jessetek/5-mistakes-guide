The Story of
Fountain Valley.

Fountain Valley is a city of about 56,036 people in Orange County.

Mile Square Regional Park, about 602 acres, is the largest of 22 parks here. Golfers have David L. Baker Golf Course and Mile Square Golf Course. Local draws include The Reptile Zoo and Sapphire Center.

The map shows 62 places to eat and drink, with Vietnamese, Coffee and Japanese the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by Fountain Valley Elementary and Garden Grove Unified. On price, Fountain Valley is the 20th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.38M for the typical home, and values are up 117% in ten years.

Fountain Valley Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Fountain Valley, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Fountain Valley is worth about $1,377,121, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Fountain Valley going up or down?

The typical home value in Fountain Valley is up 6.0% over the past year, from about $1,299,038 to about $1,377,121. Over ten years it is up 117%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Fountain Valley?

Typical values in Fountain Valley by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $456,069, 2 bedroom about $691,351, 3 bedroom about $1,239,268, 4 bedroom about $1,440,345, 5+ bedroom about $1,648,854. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Fountain Valley?

The typical asking rent in Fountain Valley is about $2,998 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Fountain Valley?

14 public schools sit inside the Fountain Valley boundary: 8 elementary, 3 middle and 3 high. They belong to Fountain Valley Elementary and Garden Grove Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Fountain Valley?

Fountain Valley has 22 parks. The largest are Mile Square Regional Park (about 602 acres), Fountain Valley Sports Park (about 81 acres) and Green Valley Park (about 19 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Fountain Valley?

Fountain Valley is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Huntington Beach, Westminster, Costa Mesa and Santa Ana.
