The Story of
La Verne.

La Verne is a city of about 30,192 people in Los Angeles County.

La Verne Sports Park, about 26 acres, is the largest of 17 parks here. Golfers have Sierra La Verne Country Club. Places people go: Edwards Theater.

University of La Verne is here too. The map shows 39 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Asian and Italian the most common. There are 6 public schools inside the boundary, run by Bonita Unified. On price, La Verne is the 56th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $934K for the typical home, and values are up 77% in ten years.

La Verne Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in La Verne, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in La Verne is worth about $934,216, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in La Verne going up or down?

The typical home value in La Verne is up 1.4% over the past year, from about $921,181 to about $934,216. Over ten years it is up 77%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in La Verne?

Typical values in La Verne by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $409,919, 2 bedroom about $554,893, 3 bedroom about $866,897, 4 bedroom about $1,039,663, 5+ bedroom about $1,391,237. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in La Verne?

The typical asking rent in La Verne is about $3,008 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in La Verne?

6 public schools sit inside the La Verne boundary: 4 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Bonita Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in La Verne?

La Verne has 17 parks. The largest are La Verne Sports Park (about 26 acres), Las Flores Park (about 17 acres) and Golden Hills Wilderness Park (about 9.2 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near La Verne?

La Verne is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are San Dimas, Claremont, Pomona and Glendora.
