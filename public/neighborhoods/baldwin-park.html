The Story of
Baldwin Park.

Baldwin Park, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 69,247 residents.

It dates to 1880. There are 4 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Morgan Park, at about 11 acres. Local draws include Frank's Market and In-N-Out Burger Museum.

The map shows 16 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Coffee and Seafood the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by Baldwin Park Unified. On price, Baldwin Park is the 23rd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $708K for the typical home, and values are up 93% in ten years.

Baldwin Park Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Baldwin Park, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Baldwin Park is worth about $708,074, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Baldwin Park going up or down?

The typical home value in Baldwin Park is up 0.6% over the past year, from about $703,573 to about $708,074. Over ten years it is up 93%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Baldwin Park?

Typical values in Baldwin Park by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $494,657, 2 bedroom about $611,125, 3 bedroom about $712,887, 4 bedroom about $790,869, 5+ bedroom about $882,030. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Baldwin Park?

The typical asking rent in Baldwin Park is about $2,959 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Baldwin Park?

14 public schools sit inside the Baldwin Park boundary: 8 elementary, 4 middle and 2 high. They belong to Baldwin Park Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Baldwin Park?

Baldwin Park has 4 parks. The largest are Morgan Park (about 11 acres), Barnes Park (about 5.5 acres) and Walnut Creek Nature Park (about 4.4 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Baldwin Park?

Baldwin Park is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Irwindale, West Puente Valley, Vincent and El Monte.
