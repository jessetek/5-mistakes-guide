The Story of
South El Monte.

About 19,121 people call South El Monte home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1958. There are 2 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Shiveley Park, at about 6.3 acres.

There are 5 public schools inside the boundary, run by Mountain View Elementary and Valle Lindo Elementary. On price, South El Monte is the 26th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $746K for the typical home, and values are up 93% in ten years.

What is the typical home value in South El Monte, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in South El Monte is worth about $745,520, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in South El Monte going up or down?

The typical home value in South El Monte is about the same as a year ago, near $745,520. Over ten years it is up 93%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in South El Monte?

Typical values in South El Monte by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $676,824, 3 bedroom about $728,384, 4 bedroom about $819,561, 5+ bedroom about $943,821. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in South El Monte?

5 public schools sit inside the South El Monte boundary: 3 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Mountain View Elementary and Valle Lindo Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in South El Monte?

South El Monte has 2 parks. The largest are Shiveley Park (about 6.3 acres) and Mary Van Dyke Park (about 1.5 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near South El Monte?

South El Monte is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are El Monte, Rosemead, Temple City and San Gabriel.
