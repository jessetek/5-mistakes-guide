The place

The Story of
La Habra.

La Habra, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 61,391 residents.

La Bonita Park, about 24 acres, is the largest of 16 parks here. Golfers have Westridge Golf Club. For a day out there is Regal La Habra Stadium 16.

The map shows 37 places to eat and drink, with American, Mexican and Coffee the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by La Habra City Elementary, Lowell Joint and Fullerton Joint Union High. On price, La Habra is the 58th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $901K for the typical home, and values are up 87% in ten years.

Why La Habra

What Makes La Habra
Worth Calling Home.

🎒

OC School District Quality

Fullerton Joint Union HSD and Lowell Joint SD both rank well. Buyers who can't afford full-OC pricing get OC schools at a discount here.

🏛️

Walkable Old Town La Habra

Real downtown with restaurants, shops, the Children's Museum, and public art. Few SoCal cities this size have an actual walkable core.

🌳

La Habra Hills Views

North La Habra rises into the Puente Hills, homes with views over the LA basin and toward the Pacific.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family-First City

Parks, rec programs, community events. La Habra leans hard into family-oriented quality of life.
