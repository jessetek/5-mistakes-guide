The Story of
Florence-Graham.

Florence-Graham, in Los Angeles County, is a unincorporated community of roughly 61,983 residents.

There are 5 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Ted Watkins Memorial Park, at about 25 acres.

There are 24 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Florence-Graham is the 2nd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $595K for the typical home, and values are up 108% in ten years.

Florence-Graham Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Florence-Graham, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Florence-Graham is worth about $594,781, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Florence-Graham going up or down?

The typical home value in Florence-Graham is up 0.6% over the past year, from about $591,171 to about $594,781. Over ten years it is up 108%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Florence-Graham?

Typical values in Florence-Graham by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $457,924, 2 bedroom about $544,856, 3 bedroom about $616,191, 4 bedroom about $676,306, 5+ bedroom about $757,817. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in Florence-Graham?

24 public schools sit inside the Florence-Graham boundary: 11 elementary, 6 middle and 7 high. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Florence-Graham?

Florence-Graham has 5 parks. The largest are Ted Watkins Memorial Park (about 25 acres), Roosevelt Park (about 23 acres) and Colonel Leo H Washington Park (about 11 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Florence-Graham?

Florence-Graham is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Walnut Park, Huntington Park, Vernon and Willowbrook.
