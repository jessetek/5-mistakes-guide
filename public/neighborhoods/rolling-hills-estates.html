The Story of
Rolling Hills Estates.

About 8,007 people call Rolling Hills Estates home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1957. Ernie J Howlett Park, about 28 acres, is the largest of 8 parks here. Open space and trails include George F Canyon Nature Preserve and Linden H. Chandler Preserve.

Local draws include Regal Promenade 13 and Tiger Woods Point. The map shows 22 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Peruvian and Mexican the most common. There are 2 public schools inside the boundary, run by Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified. On price, Rolling Hills Estates is the 8th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.86M for the typical home, and values are up 81% in ten years.
