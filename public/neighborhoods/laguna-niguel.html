The Story of
Laguna Niguel.

About 64,185 people call Laguna Niguel home. It sits in Orange County.

It has been its own community since 1989. Laguna Niguel Regional Park, about 190 acres, is the largest of 38 parks here. Places people go: Regency Theatres Director's Cut Cinema and Cinépolis.

The map shows 42 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, American and Pizza the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Capistrano Unified. On price, Laguna Niguel is the 14th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.49M for the typical home, and values are up 118% in ten years.

Laguna Niguel Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Laguna Niguel, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Laguna Niguel is worth about $1,493,093, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Laguna Niguel going up or down?

The typical home value in Laguna Niguel is up 5.0% over the past year, from about $1,421,348 to about $1,493,093. Over ten years it is up 118%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Laguna Niguel?

Typical values in Laguna Niguel by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $516,595, 2 bedroom about $797,714, 3 bedroom about $1,371,580, 4 bedroom about $1,955,311, 5+ bedroom about $2,442,553. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Laguna Niguel?

The typical asking rent in Laguna Niguel is about $3,343 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Laguna Niguel?

9 public schools sit inside the Laguna Niguel boundary: 8 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to Capistrano Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Laguna Niguel?

Laguna Niguel has 38 parks. The largest are Laguna Niguel Regional Park (about 190 acres), Crown Valley Community Park (about 43 acres) and Chapparosa Park (about 20 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Laguna Niguel?

Laguna Niguel is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are San Juan Capistrano, Laguna Beach, Aliso Viejo and Ladera Ranch.
