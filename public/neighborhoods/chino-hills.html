The Story of
Chino Hills.

Chino Hills, in San Bernardino County, is a city of roughly 77,418 residents.

It has been its own community since 1991. The name comes from Chino Hills. For green space, Chino Hills has 43 parks, led by Chino Hills Community Park at roughly 29 acres. Open space and trails include Water Canyon Natural Preserve and Tres Hermanos Ranch.

Golfers have Los Serranos Golf & Country Club and Western Hills Golf Course (Chino Hills). Local draws include Harkins Theatres. The map shows 65 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Pizza and Korean the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by Chino Valley Unified. On price, Chino Hills is the 52nd highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $973K for the typical home, and values are up 79% in ten years.

Answered.

What is the typical home value in Chino Hills, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Chino Hills is worth about $973,080, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Chino Hills going up or down?

The typical home value in Chino Hills is up 1.6% over the past year, from about $958,168 to about $973,080. Over ten years it is up 79%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Chino Hills?

Typical values in Chino Hills by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $456,394, 2 bedroom about $557,660, 3 bedroom about $813,842, 4 bedroom about $1,065,819, 5+ bedroom about $1,357,925. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Chino Hills?

The typical asking rent in Chino Hills is about $3,085 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Chino Hills?

14 public schools sit inside the Chino Hills boundary: 10 elementary, 2 middle and 2 high. They belong to Chino Valley Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Chino Hills?

Chino Hills has 43 parks. The largest are Chino Hills Community Park (about 29 acres), Veterans Park (about 24 acres) and Grand Avenue Park (about 15 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Chino Hills?

Chino Hills is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Chino, Yorba Linda, Anaheim and Diamond Bar.
