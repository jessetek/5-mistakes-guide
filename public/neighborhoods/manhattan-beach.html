The place

The Story of
Manhattan Beach.

Manhattan Beach, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 33,453 residents.

Its official start date is 1912, about 114 years ago. Polliwog Park, about 18 acres, is the largest of 8 parks here. It has ocean beach: Manhattan Beach.

Golfers have Westdrift Golf. Places people go: Manhattan Village Mall. The map shows 91 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Mexican and Italian the most common. There are 7 public schools inside the boundary, run by Manhattan Beach Unified.

Why Manhattan Beach

What Makes Manhattan Beach
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏖️

Walk Streets + The Strand

Walk Streets, pedestrian-only beach blocks, are MB's most iconic. Plus 22-mile Strand bike path. Real beach-town culture.

🏫

MBUSD Schools: Top in LA County

Mira Costa High consistently ranks in CA's top 50 public high schools. K-8 feeders are equally strong. Buyers price homes by feeder zone.

🇺🇸

Volleyball + Surf Capital

Manhattan Beach Open is the world's biggest beach volleyball event. Hometown surf culture is genuine, not cosplay.

💼

Tech + Aerospace Money

Silicon Beach + SpaceX + aerospace exec demographic keeps the $5M+ market liquid year-round.

Where to Live
in Manhattan Beach.

A real-talk breakdown of the Manhattan Beach sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

Sand Section / Walk Streets

Closest to the beach. Walk-Street blocks are quiet, no cars. Premium MB pricing.

📍

Hill Section

Hilltop east of PCH. Ocean views, larger lots, custom estates. Mid-luxury MB.

📍

Tree Section

Inland family neighborhoods. Better elementary feeders, larger yards, mid-$3M entry to MB.

📍

East Manhattan Beach

Mid-priced relative to MB. Closer to PCH and 405. Good value MB entry.
