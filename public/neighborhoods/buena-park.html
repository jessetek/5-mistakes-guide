The place

The Story of
Buena Park.

Buena Park, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 82,611 residents.

It dates to 1887. Ralph B. Clark Regional Park, about 81 acres, is the largest of 13 parks here. Golfers have Los Coyotes Country Club.

For a day out there is CGV Buena Park, Krikorian Buena Park Metroplex 18 and Calico Railroad. The map shows 84 places to eat and drink, with Korean, American and Coffee the most common. There are 12 public schools inside the boundary, run by Buena Park Elementary and Centralia Elementary. On price, Buena Park is the 57th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $929K for the typical home, and values are up 86% in ten years.

Why Buena Park

What Makes Buena Park
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

💵

OC Schools, LA Prices

Median $865K with OC school district feeders is genuinely rare. Most comparable OC areas are $1M+. Buyers who appreciate value plays love Buena Park.

🎢

The Source District + Knott's

Major commercial revitalization with The Source OC, Knott's Berry Farm, and the Buena Park Downtown all driving local foot traffic and home demand.

🛣️

5 + 91 Freeway Access

Direct 5 and 91 access. Downtown LA is 25 min, OC core is 15 min, beaches are 25 min.

🌮

Unbeatable Food Scene

Korea Town is right next door. Buena Park itself has a deep mix of Korean BBQ, Mexican, Vietnamese, and Filipino spots locals swear by.
