The Story of
Cudahy.

Cudahy, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 21,694 residents.

It has been its own community since 1960. Cudahy Park, about 8.4 acres, is the largest of 4 parks here.

There are 5 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Cudahy is the 15th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $690K for the typical home, and values are up 105% in ten years.

Cudahy Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Cudahy, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Cudahy is worth about $689,632, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Cudahy going up or down?

The typical home value in Cudahy is up 2.9% over the past year, from about $670,393 to about $689,632. Over ten years it is up 105%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Cudahy?

Typical values in Cudahy by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $644,235, 3 bedroom about $681,283, 4 bedroom about $743,109. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in Cudahy?

5 public schools sit inside the Cudahy boundary: 4 elementary and 1 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Cudahy?

Cudahy has 4 parks. The largest are Cudahy Park (about 8.4 acres), Clara Park (about 5.1 acres) and Lugo Park (about 4.2 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Cudahy?

Cudahy is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Bell, South Gate, Bell Gardens and Maywood.
