The Story of
La Cañada Flintridge.

La Cañada Flintridge, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 19,621 residents.

Its official start date is 1976, about 50 years ago. For green space, La Cañada Flintridge has 6 parks, led by Descanso Gardens at roughly 93 acres. Open space and trails include Winery Canyon Open Space and Upper Arroyo Seco. Golfers have La Cañada Flintridge Country Club.

Local draws include UA La Canada 8. The map shows 25 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, American and Sushi the most common. There are 4 public schools inside the boundary, run by La Canada Unified. On price, La Cañada Flintridge is the 5th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $2.46M for the typical home, and values are up 94% in ten years.

Why La Cañada Flintridge

What Makes La Cañada Flintridge
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏫

#1 School District in California

La Cañada Unified consistently ranks first in California. La Cañada High School is regularly a top-3 public HS in the state. Buyers literally move here for kindergarten enrollment.

🌿

Foothill Trees + Privacy

Mature trees, larger lots, hillside privacy. Real residential character, no urban density, no commercial intrusion.

🏕️

Descanso + Foothill Lifestyle

Descanso Gardens, Angeles Forest access, JPL adjacency. A foothill lifestyle hard to find elsewhere in LA.

🏘️

Mid-Century + Custom Inventory

A lot of LCF housing is 1950s-70s mid-century original or restored, with newer custom builds infilled. Distinct architectural character.
