The Story of
Azusa.

Azusa is a city of about 48,383 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1887. For green space, Azusa has 17 parks, led by Northside Park at roughly 18 acres. Open space and trails include River Wilderness Park. Golfers have Azusa Greens Country Club.

Places people go: Regency Foothill Center 10. Azusa High School is here too. The map shows 23 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Coffee and American the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Azusa Unified. On price, Azusa is the 22nd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $706K for the typical home, and values are up 82% in ten years.

Azusa Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Azusa, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Azusa is worth about $705,662, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Azusa going up or down?

The typical home value in Azusa is up 0.4% over the past year, from about $702,566 to about $705,662. Over ten years it is up 82%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Azusa?

Typical values in Azusa by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $419,805, 2 bedroom about $551,728, 3 bedroom about $699,445, 4 bedroom about $791,299, 5+ bedroom about $1,090,577. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Azusa?

The typical asking rent in Azusa is about $2,532 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Azusa?

9 public schools sit inside the Azusa boundary: 8 elementary and 1 high. They belong to Azusa Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Azusa?

Azusa has 17 parks. The largest are Northside Park (about 18 acres), Lario Staging Area (about 18 acres) and Memorial Park (about 13 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Azusa?

Azusa is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Duarte, Vincent, Bradbury and Irwindale.
