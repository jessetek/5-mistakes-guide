The Story of
Glendora.

About 50,101 people call Glendora home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

For green space, Glendora has 12 parks, led by Louie Pompei Memorial Sports Park at roughly 16 acres. Open space and trails include South Hills Wilderness Area and Big Dalton Canyon Wilderness Park. Golfers have Glen Oaks Golf & Learning Center. Places people go: AMC Glendora 12 and Haugh Performing Arts Center.

Citrus College is here too. The map shows 28 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Mexican and Pizza the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Glendora Unified and Charter Oak Unified. On price, Glendora is the 55th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $880K for the typical home, and values are up 67% in ten years.

Glendora Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Glendora, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Glendora is worth about $879,753, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Glendora going up or down?

The typical home value in Glendora is up 0.7% over the past year, from about $873,363 to about $879,753. Over ten years it is up 67%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Glendora?

Typical values in Glendora by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $549,184, 2 bedroom about $675,725, 3 bedroom about $836,705, 4 bedroom about $1,002,384, 5+ bedroom about $1,501,516. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Glendora?

The typical asking rent in Glendora is about $2,950 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Glendora?

9 public schools sit inside the Glendora boundary: 6 elementary, 2 middle and 1 high. They belong to Glendora Unified and Charter Oak Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Glendora?

Glendora has 12 parks. The largest are Louie Pompei Memorial Sports Park (about 16 acres), Sandburg Middle School Park (about 14 acres) and Finkbiner Park (about 12 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Glendora?

Glendora is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are San Dimas, Azusa, Covina and La Verne.
