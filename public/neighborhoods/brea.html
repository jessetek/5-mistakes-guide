The Story of
Brea.

About 47,844 people call Brea home. It sits in Orange County.

There are 13 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Tracks at Brea, at about 25 acres. Golfers have Birch Hills Golf Course and Brea Creek Golf Course. Local draws include Brea Mall and Brea Edwards Cinema.

The map shows 72 places to eat and drink, with American, Coffee and Mexican the most common. There are 8 public schools inside the boundary, run by Brea-Olinda Unified. On price, Brea is the 37th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.12M for the typical home, and values are up 77% in ten years.

Why Brea

What Makes Brea
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🎓

Brea Olinda Unified: Top OC District

Brea schools rank in the top 10% statewide. School quality is the single biggest driver of Brea home values. Buyers shop districts as much as homes.

🛍️

Real Walkable Downtown Brea

Brea's downtown district is a genuine destination, restaurants, the Improv, the Brea Mall, and a real walkability factor that's rare in OC.

⛰️

Hillside Character + Views

North Brea rises into the Puente Hills, homes with city/canyon views, larger lots, custom architecture. Different vibe than tract OC.

📈

Tight Inventory, Strong Demand

Small city, no big tracts left to develop, schools driving consistent demand. Inventory rarely lasts long here.
