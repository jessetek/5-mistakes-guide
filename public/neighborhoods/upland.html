The Story of
Upland.

Upland, in San Bernardino County, is a city of roughly 79,684 residents.

Upland Memorial Park, about 40 acres, is the largest of 13 parks here. Golfers have Upland Hills Country Club. For a day out there is Upland Highlander Stadium, Bullwinkle's and Cooper Regional History Museum.

The map shows 91 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Pizza and American the most common. There are 15 public schools inside the boundary, run by Upland Unified. On price, Upland is the 44th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $814K for the typical home, and values are up 73% in ten years.

Upland Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Upland, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Upland is worth about $813,655, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Upland going up or down?

The typical home value in Upland is up 1.3% over the past year, from about $803,499 to about $813,655. Over ten years it is up 73%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Upland?

Typical values in Upland by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $498,246, 2 bedroom about $550,374, 3 bedroom about $748,386, 4 bedroom about $921,565, 5+ bedroom about $1,113,851. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Upland?

The typical asking rent in Upland is about $2,392 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Upland?

15 public schools sit inside the Upland boundary: 10 elementary, 2 middle, 2 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Upland Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Upland?

Upland has 13 parks. The largest are Upland Memorial Park (about 40 acres), Cabrillo Park (about 20 acres) and San Antonio Park (about 10 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Upland?

Upland is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Claremont, Montclair, Rancho Cucamonga and Ontario.
