The place

The Story of
Glendale.

Glendale is a city of about 187,823 people in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1884. There are 26 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Crescenta Valley Park, at about 37 acres. Open space and trails include Deukmejian Wilderness Park and Brand Park. Golfers have Oakmont Country Club and Scholl Canyon Golf Course.

Local draws include The Americana at Brand, Alex Theatre and Laemmle Glendale. Glendale Community College is here too. The map shows 206 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Pizza and Coffee the most common. There are 23 public schools inside the boundary, run by Glendale Unified. On price, Glendale is the 30th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.17M for the typical home, and values are up 92% in ten years.

Why Glendale

What Makes Glendale
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

⛰️

Verdugo Hills Hillside Living

North Glendale rises into the Verdugos, homes with hillside character, views, and large lot sizes uncommon in LA.

🇦🇲

Strong Armenian-American Community

One of the largest Armenian-American populations in the US. Restaurants, businesses, churches, and a deep cultural identity.

🛍️

Americana at Brand + Glendale Galleria

Two major retail/dining anchors. Walkable, modern, well-connected, a real downtown experience few LA suburbs offer.

📍

Central LA Location

Direct 134, 5, 2 freeway access. Downtown LA in 15 min, Pasadena in 10, Burbank studios in 5.
