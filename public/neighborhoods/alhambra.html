The Story of
Alhambra.

About 80,279 people call Alhambra home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from Alhambra. There are 8 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Almansor Park, at about 27 acres. Golfers have Alhambra Golf Course. For a day out there is Regal Edwards Alhambra Renaissance.

Alhambra Medical University is here too. The map shows 115 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Boba tea and Vietnamese the most common. There are 12 public schools inside the boundary, run by Alhambra Unified. On price, Alhambra is the 61st highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $911K for the typical home, and values are up 79% in ten years.

Answered.

What is the typical home value in Alhambra, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Alhambra is worth about $911,097, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Alhambra going up or down?

The typical home value in Alhambra is up 1.3% over the past year, from about $899,762 to about $911,097. Over ten years it is up 79%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Alhambra?

Typical values in Alhambra by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $539,265, 2 bedroom about $784,938, 3 bedroom about $947,683, 4 bedroom about $1,107,764, 5+ bedroom about $1,292,697. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Alhambra?

The typical asking rent in Alhambra is about $2,645 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Alhambra?

12 public schools sit inside the Alhambra boundary: 9 elementary and 3 high. They belong to Alhambra Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Alhambra?

Alhambra has 8 parks. The largest are Almansor Park (about 27 acres), Moor Field (about 19 acres) and Granada Park (about 15 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Alhambra?

Alhambra is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are South Pasadena, San Gabriel, Monterey Park and San Marino.
