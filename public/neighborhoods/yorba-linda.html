The place

The Story of
Yorba Linda.

About 66,489 people call Yorba Linda home. It sits in Orange County.

It dates to 1967. Carbon Canyon Regional Park, about 202 acres, is the largest of 22 parks here. Open space and trails include Arroyo Park. Golfers have Black Gold Golf Club.

Places people go: Richard Nixon Presidential Library & Museum. The map shows 29 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Mexican and Pizza the most common. There are 10 public schools inside the boundary, run by Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified. On price, Yorba Linda is the 17th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.4M for the typical home, and values are up 89% in ten years.

Why Yorba Linda

What Makes Yorba Linda
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏫

Placentia-Yorba Linda USD

PYLUSD is one of OC's top districts. Yorba Linda HS, Esperanza HS, and feeder elementaries all rate in the 8-10/10 range.

⛰️

Hillside + Ranch Land

Yorba Linda has more genuine ranch / equestrian property than most OC cities, actual horse trails, larger lots, custom estates.

🏘️

Strong Custom-Build Stock

Significant share of the inventory is 90s-2010s custom builds on quarter-acre+ lots. Quality construction, low rebuilds.

💎

Low Inventory, Stable Prices

Yorba Linda turns over slower than most OC cities. Once people land here, they stay. Sellers benefit from supply scarcity.
