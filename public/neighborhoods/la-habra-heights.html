La Habra Heights.

La Habra Heights, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 5,382 residents.

Its official start date is 1978, about 48 years ago. Hacienda Park is the main park here.

Open space and trails include Puente Hills Preserve. Golfers have Hacienda Golf Club. On price, La Habra Heights is the 19th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.38M for the typical home, and values are up 78% in ten years.
