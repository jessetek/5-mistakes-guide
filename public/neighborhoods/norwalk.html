The place

The Story of
Norwalk.

About 98,230 people call Norwalk home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1957. There are 13 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Holifield Park, at about 16 acres. Golfers have Don Knabe Golf Center & Junior Academy. Local draws include Regency Norwalk 8 and AMC Norwalk 20.

Cerritos College is here too. The map shows 12 places to eat and drink. There are 21 public schools inside the boundary, run by Norwalk-La Mirada Unified and Little Lake City Elementary. On price, Norwalk is the 32nd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $754K for the typical home, and values are up 92% in ten years.

Why Norwalk

What Makes Norwalk
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

💰

Affordable Entry Into LA County

One of the last LA cities where a working couple can still realistically buy a single-family home. Median is well below the LA County average.

🛣️

Best-in-Class Freeway Access

5, 605, and 105 all converge here. Downtown LA is 25 min, OC line is 10 min, LAX is 35. Hard to beat for commuters.

🚉

Metro Green Line Stop

Norwalk/Studebaker station puts you on the rail system. Future Metro expansion projects keep adding value.

🌮

Real Norwalk Food

Birrieria San Marcos, Tacos La Estrella, La Huasteca. Authentic mariscos and birria spots without the Yelp crowd or the Westside markup.

Where to Live
in Norwalk.

A real-talk breakdown of the Norwalk sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

North Norwalk

Older single-family stock, larger lots, established trees. Best-value zip in the city.

📍

Imperial Hwy / Studebaker corridor

Walking distance to the Metro Green Line. Mix of SFR and townhomes.

📍

South Norwalk / La Mirada border

Quieter, family-oriented, slight premium for proximity to La Mirada schools.

📍

Bloomfield Ave area

Commercial corridor with denser housing and condo options.
