South Whittier.

South Whittier, in Los Angeles County, is a unincorporated community of roughly 56,415 residents.

For green space, South Whittier has 2 parks, led by Adventure Park at roughly 15 acres. Golfers have Candlewood Country Club.

Local draws include Mayberry Park Splash Pad. There are 11 public schools inside the boundary, run by East Whittier City Elementary and South Whittier Elementary.

How many public schools are in South Whittier?

11 public schools sit inside the South Whittier boundary: 8 elementary, 2 middle and 1 high. They belong to East Whittier City Elementary and South Whittier Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in South Whittier?

South Whittier has 2 parks. The largest are Adventure Park (about 15 acres) and Amelia Mayberry Park (about 14 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.
