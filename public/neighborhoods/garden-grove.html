The place

The Story of
Garden Grove.

Garden Grove, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 172,361 residents.

It has been its own community since 1874. There are 20 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Garden Grove Park, at about 31 acres. Golfers have The Navy Golf Course Seal Beach. Local draws include Regal Garden Grove and Starlight Cinemas West Grove.

Concorde Career College - Garden Grove is here too. The map shows 148 places to eat and drink, with Vietnamese, Coffee and Boba tea the most common. There are 46 public schools inside the boundary, run by Garden Grove Unified and Westminster. On price, Garden Grove is the 49th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.0M for the typical home, and values are up 106% in ten years.

Why Garden Grove

What Makes Garden Grove
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🍜

Little Saigon Culinary Capital

The Bolsa Avenue corridor is a regional food destination. Vietnamese, Chinese, Korean, and Thai food at the highest concentration in SoCal.

📍

Central OC Location

22, 5, 405, 57, 22 freeways all serve. Disneyland 10 min, beaches 20 min, downtown LA 35 min.

🏘️

Mid-Priced OC Family Market

Median $885K, accessible OC pricing in a city with real amenities. First-time and step-up buyers both find homes here.

🌳

Real Park System

Garden Grove Park, Eastgate Park, and the Civic Center area give the city more green space than most OC cities of comparable size.
