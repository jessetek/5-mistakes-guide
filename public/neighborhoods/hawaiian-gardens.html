What is the typical home value in Hawaiian Gardens, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Hawaiian Gardens is worth about $644,400, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Hawaiian Gardens going up or down?

The typical home value in Hawaiian Gardens is up 2.8% over the past year, from about $627,139 to about $644,400. Over ten years it is up 107%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Hawaiian Gardens?

Typical values in Hawaiian Gardens by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $406,041, 2 bedroom about $540,829, 3 bedroom about $700,667, 4 bedroom about $855,746. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in Hawaiian Gardens?

3 public schools sit inside the Hawaiian Gardens boundary: 2 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to ABC Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.
