The Story of
Laguna Hills.

Laguna Hills, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 30,378 residents.

It dates to 1991. For green space, Laguna Hills has 18 parks, led by Aliso Creek Open Space at roughly 32 acres. Local draws include Laguna Hills Farmer's Market.

The map shows 29 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, American and Seafood the most common. There are 4 public schools inside the boundary, run by Saddleback Valley Unified. On price, Laguna Hills is the 26th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.23M for the typical home, and values are up 98% in ten years.

Laguna Hills Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Laguna Hills, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Laguna Hills is worth about $1,228,966, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Laguna Hills going up or down?

The typical home value in Laguna Hills is up 4.4% over the past year, from about $1,176,858 to about $1,228,966. Over ten years it is up 98%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Laguna Hills?

Typical values in Laguna Hills by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $488,464, 2 bedroom about $652,640, 3 bedroom about $1,009,975, 4 bedroom about $1,601,149, 5+ bedroom about $2,522,695. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Laguna Hills?

The typical asking rent in Laguna Hills is about $3,029 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Laguna Hills?

4 public schools sit inside the Laguna Hills boundary: 2 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Saddleback Valley Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Laguna Hills?

Laguna Hills has 18 parks. The largest are Aliso Creek Open Space (about 32 acres), Laguna Hills Community Center & Sports Complex (about 11 acres) and Moulton Ranch Park (about 7.2 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Laguna Hills?

Laguna Hills is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Aliso Viejo, Laguna Woods, Mission Viejo and Laguna Niguel.
