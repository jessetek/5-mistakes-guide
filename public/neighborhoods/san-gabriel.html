The Story of
San Gabriel.

San Gabriel is a city of about 37,926 people in Los Angeles County.

For green space, San Gabriel has 3 parks, led by Vincent Lugo Park at roughly 9.7 acres. Golfers have San Gabriel Country Club. Places people go: San Gabriel Mission Playhouse.

The map shows 132 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Boba tea and Taiwanese the most common. There are 8 public schools inside the boundary, run by San Gabriel Unified. On price, San Gabriel is the 42nd highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.09M for the typical home, and values are up 76% in ten years.

San Gabriel Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in San Gabriel, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in San Gabriel is worth about $1,085,609, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in San Gabriel going up or down?

The typical home value in San Gabriel is up 2.7% over the past year, from about $1,057,423 to about $1,085,609. Over ten years it is up 76%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in San Gabriel?

Typical values in San Gabriel by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $716,054, 2 bedroom about $906,180, 3 bedroom about $1,071,850, 4 bedroom about $1,291,409, 5+ bedroom about $1,631,218. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in San Gabriel?

The typical asking rent in San Gabriel is about $2,706 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in San Gabriel?

8 public schools sit inside the San Gabriel boundary: 5 elementary, 1 middle and 2 high. They belong to San Gabriel Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in San Gabriel?

San Gabriel has 3 parks. The largest are Vincent Lugo Park (about 9.7 acres), Smith Park (about 5.5 acres) and Plaza Park (about 0.9 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near San Gabriel?

San Gabriel is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are East San Gabriel, Rosemead, San Marino and Alhambra.
