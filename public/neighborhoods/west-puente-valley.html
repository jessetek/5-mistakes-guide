The Story of
West Puente Valley.

West Puente Valley, in Los Angeles County, is a unincorporated community of roughly 22,959 residents.

For green space, West Puente Valley has 2 parks, led by Bassett Park at roughly 9.9 acres.

There are 6 public schools inside the boundary, run by Bassett Unified and Hacienda la Puente Unified. On price, West Puente Valley is the 25th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $745K for the typical home, and values are up 93% in ten years.

West Puente Valley Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in West Puente Valley, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in West Puente Valley is worth about $745,369, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in West Puente Valley going up or down?

The typical home value in West Puente Valley is up 1.4% over the past year, from about $734,925 to about $745,369. Over ten years it is up 93%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in West Puente Valley?

Typical values in West Puente Valley by bedroom count: 3 bedroom about $734,771, 4 bedroom about $755,411, 5+ bedroom about $850,234. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in West Puente Valley?

6 public schools sit inside the West Puente Valley boundary: 5 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to Bassett Unified and Hacienda la Puente Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in West Puente Valley?

West Puente Valley has 2 parks. The largest are Bassett Park (about 9.9 acres) and Allen J Martin Park (about 6.4 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near West Puente Valley?

West Puente Valley is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are La Puente, Baldwin Park, Valinda and Industry.
