Los Alamitos, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 11,846 residents.

Los Alamitos Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Los Alamitos, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Los Alamitos is worth about $1,568,950, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Los Alamitos going up or down?

The typical home value in Los Alamitos is up 5.5% over the past year, from about $1,487,382 to about $1,568,950. Over ten years it is up 94%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Los Alamitos?

Typical values in Los Alamitos by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $832,369, 3 bedroom about $1,485,404, 4 bedroom about $1,743,404, 5+ bedroom about $2,097,511. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Los Alamitos?

The typical asking rent in Los Alamitos is about $3,143 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Los Alamitos?

4 public schools sit inside the Los Alamitos boundary: 1 elementary, 2 middle and 1 high. They belong to Los Alamitos Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Los Alamitos?

Los Alamitos has 8 parks. The largest are Oak Academy Park (about 12 acres), Arbor Park (about 8.3 acres) and Little Cottonwood Park (about 6.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Los Alamitos?

Los Alamitos is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Cypress, Hawaiian Gardens, Seal Beach and Stanton.
