The Story of
Stanton.

About 41,188 people call Stanton home. It sits in Orange County.

Its official start date is 1956, about 70 years ago. There are 4 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Stanton Central Park, at about 11 acres. For a day out there is Adventure City.

The map shows 20 places to eat and drink, with Coffee and Boba tea the most common. There are 3 public schools inside the boundary, run by Magnolia Elementary. On price, Stanton is the 40th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $785K for the typical home, and values are up 95% in ten years.

Stanton Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Stanton, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Stanton is worth about $784,807, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Stanton going up or down?

The typical home value in Stanton is up 2.1% over the past year, from about $768,477 to about $784,807. Over ten years it is up 95%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Stanton?

Typical values in Stanton by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $450,531, 2 bedroom about $589,254, 3 bedroom about $832,292, 4 bedroom about $940,828, 5+ bedroom about $1,095,971. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Stanton?

The typical asking rent in Stanton is about $2,683 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Stanton?

3 public schools sit inside the Stanton boundary: 3 elementary. They belong to Magnolia Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Stanton?

Stanton has 4 parks. The largest are Stanton Central Park (about 11 acres), Hollenbeck Park (about 11 acres) and Stanton Park (about 4.8 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Stanton?

Stanton is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Garden Grove, Cypress, Westminster and Los Alamitos.
