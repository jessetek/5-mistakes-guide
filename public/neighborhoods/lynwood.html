Lynwood Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Lynwood, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Lynwood is worth about $687,316, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Lynwood going up or down?

The typical home value in Lynwood is up 3.0% over the past year, from about $667,620 to about $687,316. Over ten years it is up 99%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Lynwood?

Typical values in Lynwood by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $522,251, 2 bedroom about $634,364, 3 bedroom about $706,926, 4 bedroom about $755,211, 5+ bedroom about $836,614. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Lynwood?

The typical asking rent in Lynwood is about $2,045 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Lynwood?

17 public schools sit inside the Lynwood boundary: 13 elementary, 2 middle and 2 high. They belong to Lynwood Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Lynwood?

Lynwood has 8 parks. The largest are Yvonne Burke-John D. Ham Park (about 9.7 acres), Adolfo Medina Park (about 2.2 acres) and Rose Park (about 1.5 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Lynwood?

Lynwood is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are South Gate, Willowbrook, East Rancho Dominguez and Compton.
