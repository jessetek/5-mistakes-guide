The place

The Story of
Hermosa Beach.

About 18,687 people call Hermosa Beach home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1907. For green space, Hermosa Beach has 9 parks, led by Valley Park at roughly 8.7 acres. It has ocean beach: Hermosa City Beach.

Local draws include Hermosa Beach Historical Society Museum, Potter's Ensemble and Hermosa Beach Playhouse. The map shows 65 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, American and Pizza the most common. There are 3 public schools inside the boundary, run by Hermosa Beach City Elementary.

Why Hermosa Beach

What Makes Hermosa Beach
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏖️

Walkable Beach Town in 1.4 Sq Mi

You can walk from one end of Hermosa to the other. Real density without losing the beach feel, most authentic of the South Bay trio.

🏐

Volleyball Capital

Hermosa Pier is the spiritual home of beach volleyball. AVP tournaments, kids' clinics, casual play every weekend.

🍸

Pier Plaza Nightlife

Restaurants and bars walkable from the beach. Younger demographic than Manhattan, more weekend energy.

🏫

HBCSD K-8 + Mira Costa Feeder

K-8 is Hermosa City School District (well-rated). High school feeds Mira Costa, top public HS in LA.
