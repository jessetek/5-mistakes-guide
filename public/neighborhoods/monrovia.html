Monrovia Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Monrovia, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Monrovia is worth about $973,541, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Monrovia going up or down?

The typical home value in Monrovia is up 1.9% over the past year, from about $955,528 to about $973,541. Over ten years it is up 66%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Monrovia?

Typical values in Monrovia by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $626,846, 2 bedroom about $789,761, 3 bedroom about $1,003,716, 4 bedroom about $1,156,655, 5+ bedroom about $1,569,437. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Monrovia?

The typical asking rent in Monrovia is about $2,822 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Monrovia?

8 public schools sit inside the Monrovia boundary: 5 elementary, 1 middle, 1 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Monrovia Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Monrovia?

Monrovia has 6 parks. The largest are Recreation Park (about 20 acres), Library Park (about 4.5 acres) and Grand Avenue Park (about 2.4 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Monrovia?

Monrovia is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Bradbury, Duarte, Arcadia and Irwindale.
