The Story of
Inglewood.

Inglewood is a city of about 102,774 people in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1888. For green space, Inglewood has 10 parks, led by Edward Vincent Jr. Park at roughly 49 acres. Places people go: SoFi Stadium, Randy's Donuts and Centinela Adobe.

The map shows 29 places to eat and drink, with Mexican and Pizza the most common. There are 20 public schools inside the boundary, run by Inglewood Unified. On price, Inglewood is the 29th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $751K for the typical home, and values are up 108% in ten years.

Inglewood Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Inglewood, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Inglewood is worth about $750,676, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Inglewood going up or down?

The typical home value in Inglewood is up 1.8% over the past year, from about $737,754 to about $750,676. Over ten years it is up 108%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Inglewood?

Typical values in Inglewood by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $415,271, 2 bedroom about $632,782, 3 bedroom about $798,659, 4 bedroom about $921,807, 5+ bedroom about $1,027,582. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Inglewood?

The typical asking rent in Inglewood is about $2,487 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Inglewood?

20 public schools sit inside the Inglewood boundary: 11 elementary, 3 middle and 6 high. They belong to Inglewood Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Inglewood?

Inglewood has 10 parks. The largest are Edward Vincent Jr. Park (about 49 acres), Darby Park (about 15 acres) and Rogers Park (about 9.2 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Inglewood?

Inglewood is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Lennox, Westmont, Hawthorne and Lawndale.
