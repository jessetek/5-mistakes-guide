The place

The Story of
Diamond Bar.

About 52,292 people call Diamond Bar home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1989. Sycamore Canyon Park, about 48 acres, is the largest of 12 parks here. Golfers have Diamond Bar Golf Course.

The map shows 53 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Pizza and Coffee the most common. There are 11 public schools inside the boundary, run by Walnut Valley Unified and Pomona Unified. On price, Diamond Bar is the 47th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.0M for the typical home, and values are up 70% in ten years.

Why Diamond Bar

What Makes Diamond Bar
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🎓

Walnut Valley Unified: Top-Tier District

Walnut Valley USD ranks among the top 10% of California districts. Diamond Bar High School consistently posts top API scores. Schools are the single biggest demand driver here.

⛰️

Hillside Community Feel

Diamond Bar was built into the hills with curving residential streets, mature landscaping, and views over the SGV. Different vibe than gridded LA cities.

🛡️

Among the Safest in LA County

Consistently ranks in the top 5 for LA County safety. Master-planned in the 60s with intentional zoning.

🛣️

Multi-County Freeway Access

60, 57, and 71 freeways converge here. Inland Empire, OC, downtown LA all within 30 minutes.
