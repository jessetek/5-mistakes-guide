What is the typical home value in La Crescenta-Montrose, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in La Crescenta-Montrose is worth about $1,280,450, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

The typical home value in La Crescenta-Montrose is up 0.7% over the past year, from about $1,271,148 to about $1,280,450. Over ten years it is up 81%.
