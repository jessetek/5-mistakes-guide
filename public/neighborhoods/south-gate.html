The Story of
South Gate.

About 90,805 people call South Gate home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1923. South Gate Park, about 85 acres, is the largest of 11 parks here. Golfers have South Gate Golf Course.

For a day out there is Regal Edwards South Gate & IMAX. The map shows 29 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, American and Mexican the most common. There are 24 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, South Gate is the 20th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $697K for the typical home, and values are up 91% in ten years.

What is the typical home value in South Gate, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in South Gate is worth about $696,567, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in South Gate going up or down?

The typical home value in South Gate is up 2.0% over the past year, from about $682,655 to about $696,567. Over ten years it is up 91%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in South Gate?

Typical values in South Gate by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $558,518, 2 bedroom about $646,115, 3 bedroom about $720,751, 4 bedroom about $772,740, 5+ bedroom about $856,365. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in South Gate?

The typical asking rent in South Gate is about $2,291 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in South Gate?

24 public schools sit inside the South Gate boundary: 16 elementary, 2 middle, 4 high and 2 k to 12. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in South Gate?

South Gate has 11 parks. The largest are South Gate Park (about 85 acres), Hollydale Regional Park (about 50 acres) and Urban Orchard Park (about 7.5 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near South Gate?

South Gate is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Lynwood, Cudahy, Walnut Park and Bell.
