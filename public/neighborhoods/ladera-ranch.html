Ladera Ranch Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Ladera Ranch, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Ladera Ranch is worth about $1,374,732, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Ladera Ranch going up or down?

The typical home value in Ladera Ranch is up 2.0% over the past year, from about $1,347,621 to about $1,374,732. Over ten years it is up 93%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Ladera Ranch?

Typical values in Ladera Ranch by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $764,723, 3 bedroom about $1,019,651, 4 bedroom about $1,719,156, 5+ bedroom about $2,309,919. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Ladera Ranch?

The typical asking rent in Ladera Ranch is about $3,550 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Ladera Ranch?

4 public schools sit inside the Ladera Ranch boundary: 3 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to Capistrano Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Ladera Ranch?

Ladera Ranch has 12 parks. The largest are Founder's Park (about 13 acres), Oso Grande Park (about 11 acres) and Chaparral Park (about 4.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Ladera Ranch?

Ladera Ranch is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are San Juan Capistrano, Laguna Niguel, Mission Viejo and Laguna Hills.
