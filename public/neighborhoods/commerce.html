Commerce Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Commerce, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Commerce is worth about $694,535, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Commerce going up or down?

The typical home value in Commerce is up 1.7% over the past year, from about $683,171 to about $694,535. Over ten years it is up 92%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Commerce?

Typical values in Commerce by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $622,988, 3 bedroom about $712,861, 4 bedroom about $779,070. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in Commerce?

2 public schools sit inside the Commerce boundary: 2 elementary. They belong to Montebello Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Commerce?

Commerce has 4 parks. The largest are Bristow Park (about 11 acres), Veterans Memorial Park (about 9.6 acres) and Rosewood Park (about 4.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Commerce?

Commerce is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Bell, Bell Gardens, Maywood and Montebello.
