The place

The Story of
Irvine.

Irvine, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 318,683 residents.

Its official start date is 1971, about 55 years ago. The name comes from James Harvey Irvine, Sr.. Great Park Sports Complex, about 237 acres, is the largest of 206 parks here. Open space and trails include San Joaquin Marsh Reserve and William R. Mason Regional Park (wilderness area). Golfers have Strawberry Farms Golf Club and Oak Creek Golf Course (Irvine).

Places people go: Wild Rivers, Anteater Stadium and Championship Soccer Stadium. Irvine Valley College is here too. The map shows 266 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, American and Asian the most common. There are 47 public schools inside the boundary, run by Irvine Unified and Tustin Unified. On price, Irvine is the 15th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.48M for the typical home, and values are up 104% in ten years.

Why Irvine

What Makes Irvine
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏫

#1 Schools in California

Irvine Unified is consistently the highest-rated district in CA. Buyers literally pick streets based on which elementary feeder zone they are in.

🏘️

Village Living

Irvine's villages. Woodbury, Northpark, Northwood, Quail Hill, each have their own personality, parks, pools, and HOA culture. Pick wrong and you will regret it.

💼

Job Magnet

Irvine Spectrum, Irvine Business Complex, the Sand Canyon biotech corridor, UC Irvine, easily one of the top 5 job hubs in SoCal. Walkable office workers drive demand.

🛡️

Safety + Stability

Year after year, the safest big city in America. That stability is priced in, but it is also why the market never crashes hard here.

Where to Live
in Irvine.

A real-talk breakdown of the Irvine sub-areas, from premium zones to value plays.

📍

Woodbury

Premium master-planned village. Resort-style amenities, top elementary feeders, late-2000s/2010s product.

📍

Northwood

Established 80s-90s neighborhood. Mature trees, larger lots, better value than newer villages.

📍

Quail Hill

Hilltop village near the I-405. Newer construction, closer to Irvine Spectrum, popular with younger families.

📍

Turtle Rock

Mountainside views, custom estates, top-tier, Irvine's quietest premium pocket near UCI.
