Valinda Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Valinda, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Valinda is worth about $764,062, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Valinda going up or down?

The typical home value in Valinda is up 0.3% over the past year, from about $762,047 to about $764,062. Over ten years it is up 88%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Valinda?

Typical values in Valinda by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $685,582, 3 bedroom about $749,499, 4 bedroom about $787,811, 5+ bedroom about $884,618. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in Valinda?

4 public schools sit inside the Valinda boundary: 3 elementary and 1 middle. They belong to Hacienda la Puente Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What cities are near Valinda?

Valinda is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Industry, La Puente, West Covina and West Puente Valley.
