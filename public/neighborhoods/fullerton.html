The place

The Story of
Fullerton.

Fullerton, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 140,054 residents.

It dates to 1887. The name comes from George H. Fullerton. There are 43 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Craig Regional Park, at about 165 acres. Open space and trails include Robert E. Ward Nature Preserve. Golfers have Coyote Hills Golf Course and Fullerton Municipal Golf Course.

Local draws include AMC Dine-In Fullerton 20, Fox Theatre and Muckenthaler Cultural Center. Fullerton College is here too. The map shows 138 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Sushi and American the most common. There are 24 public schools inside the boundary, run by Fullerton Elementary and Fullerton Joint Union High. On price, Fullerton is the 45th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.04M for the typical home, and values are up 83% in ten years.

Why Fullerton

What Makes Fullerton
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏛️

Real Walkable Downtown

Fullerton has one of the few legitimate downtown districts in north OC, restaurants, music venues, vintage shops, the Fullerton Farmers Market.

🎓

Strong Schools + Cal State Fullerton

Fullerton Joint Union HSD ranks well, plus Cal State Fullerton's presence sustains a constant rental demand and educated population.

🏠

Architectural Variety

Spanish Revival, 1920s Craftsman, mid-century modern, post-war ranch. Few OC cities have this depth of housing stock variety.

🌳

Hillside + Flatland Mix

North Fullerton hillside homes vs South Fullerton walkable flats give buyers real choice within one city.
