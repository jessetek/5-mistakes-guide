The Story of
Arcadia.

Arcadia, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 54,472 residents.

For green space, Arcadia has 16 parks, led by Los Angeles County Arboretum at roughly 115 acres. Open space and trails include Santa Anita Habitat Mitigation Project. Golfers have Santa Anita Golf Course.

For a day out there is Santa Anita Park, The Shops at Santa Anita and AMC Santa Anita 16. The map shows 106 places to eat and drink, with Chinese, Boba tea and Coffee the most common. There are 10 public schools inside the boundary, run by Arcadia Unified. On price, Arcadia is the 21st highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.38M for the typical home, and values are up 55% in ten years.

Arcadia Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Arcadia, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Arcadia is worth about $1,375,605, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Arcadia going up or down?

The typical home value in Arcadia is up 1.6% over the past year, from about $1,353,311 to about $1,375,605. Over ten years it is up 55%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Arcadia?

Typical values in Arcadia by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $822,067, 3 bedroom about $1,204,479, 4 bedroom about $1,736,855, 5+ bedroom about $2,775,729. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Arcadia?

The typical asking rent in Arcadia is about $3,412 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Arcadia?

10 public schools sit inside the Arcadia boundary: 6 elementary, 3 middle and 1 high. They belong to Arcadia Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Arcadia?

Arcadia has 16 parks. The largest are Los Angeles County Arboretum (about 115 acres), Arcadia County Park (about 44 acres) and Peck Road Water Conservation Park (about 31 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Arcadia?

Arcadia is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Temple City, Sierra Madre, East San Gabriel and Monrovia.
