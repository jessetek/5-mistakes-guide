The place

The Story of
Pico Rivera.

Pico Rivera is a city of about 59,363 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1958. There are 6 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Smith Park, at about 16 acres. Places people go: Cinepolis.

The map shows 15 places to eat and drink, with Steakhouse and American the most common. There are 13 public schools inside the boundary, run by El Rancho Unified. On price, Pico Rivera is the 27th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $747K for the typical home, and values are up 93% in ten years.

Why Pico Rivera

What Makes Pico Rivera
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏡

Affordable Family Homes

Pico Rivera offers some of the best price-per-square-foot in Southeast LA. Most homes are 1,400-2,000 sqft on 5,000-6,000 sqft lots.

🌳

Family-First Vibe

Smith Park, Rio Hondo Trail, the El Rancho schools. Pico Rivera is built for families who want a backyard and a community.

🌮

Cultura Rica

From the El Rancho swap meet to the Sunday flea market, Pico Rivera's Latino roots run deep, and so does the food.
