The Story of
Compton.

Compton is a city of about 91,129 people in Los Angeles County.

Gonzales Park, about 13 acres, is the largest of 13 parks here. For a day out there is Gateway Towne Center, Intra Market and Tomorrow's Aeronautical Museum. Compton College is here too.

The map shows 10 places to eat and drink. There are 28 public schools inside the boundary, run by Compton Unified. On price, Compton is the 3rd most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $638K for the typical home, and values are up 109% in ten years.

Compton Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Compton, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Compton is worth about $638,224, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Compton going up or down?

The typical home value in Compton is up 2.6% over the past year, from about $621,879 to about $638,224. Over ten years it is up 109%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Compton?

Typical values in Compton by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $460,473, 2 bedroom about $576,748, 3 bedroom about $654,124, 4 bedroom about $705,145, 5+ bedroom about $769,966. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Compton?

The typical asking rent in Compton is about $2,314 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Compton?

28 public schools sit inside the Compton boundary: 17 elementary, 4 middle, 5 high and 2 k to 12. They belong to Compton Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Compton?

Compton has 13 parks. The largest are Gonzales Park (about 13 acres), Burrel McDonald Park (about 4.9 acres) and Doctor Walter R Tucker Park (about 4.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Compton?

Compton is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are East Rancho Dominguez, Willowbrook, West Rancho Dominguez and Lynwood.
