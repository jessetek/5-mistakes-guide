The Story of
Laguna Woods.

Laguna Woods is a city of about 17,058 people in Orange County.

Aliso Creek Park and Wildlife Sanctuary, about 14 acres, is the largest of 3 parks here. Open space and trails include James Dilley Greenbelt Preserve.

Golfers have Laguna Woods Golf Course. There is 1 public school inside the boundary, run by Orange County Department of Education. On price, Laguna Woods is the 1st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $459K for the typical home, and values are up 77% in ten years.

    What is the typical home value in Laguna Woods, CA?

    As of September 2026, the typical home in Laguna Woods is worth about $458,736, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

    Are home values in Laguna Woods going up or down?

    The typical home value in Laguna Woods is up 3.9% over the past year, from about $441,697 to about $458,736. Over ten years it is up 77%.

    How much is a 3-bedroom home in Laguna Woods?

    Typical values in Laguna Woods by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $278,618, 2 bedroom about $452,492, 3 bedroom about $902,004. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

    What is the typical rent in Laguna Woods?

    The typical asking rent in Laguna Woods is about $3,081 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

    How many public schools are in Laguna Woods?

    1 public schools sit inside the Laguna Woods boundary: 1 k to 12. They belong to Orange County Department of Education. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

    What are the biggest parks in Laguna Woods?

    Laguna Woods has 3 parks. The largest are Aliso Creek Park and Wildlife Sanctuary (about 14 acres), Garden Center 1 (about 2.9 acres) and City Centre Park (about 0.7 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.
