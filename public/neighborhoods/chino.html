The Story of
Chino.

About 94,010 people call Chino home. It sits in San Bernardino County.

Its official start date is 1910, about 116 years ago. For green space, Chino has 36 parks, led by Prado Regional Park at roughly 258 acres. Open space and trails include The Nature Preserve and Burrowing Owl Preserve. Golfers have El Prado Golf Courses.

Places people go: Cinemark Movies 8, Planes of Fame Air Museum and Yorba-Slaughter Adobe. Chaffey College - Chino Campus is here too. The map shows 62 places to eat and drink, with Pizza, Sushi and Chinese the most common. There are 16 public schools inside the boundary, run by Chino Valley Unified. On price, Chino is the 28th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $747K for the typical home, and values are up 72% in ten years.

Chino Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Chino, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Chino is worth about $746,933, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Chino going up or down?

The typical home value in Chino is down 0.3% over the past year, from about $749,496 to about $746,933. Over ten years it is up 72%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Chino?

Typical values in Chino by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $380,630, 2 bedroom about $498,953, 3 bedroom about $696,859, 4 bedroom about $803,722, 5+ bedroom about $987,412. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Chino?

The typical asking rent in Chino is about $3,286 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Chino?

16 public schools sit inside the Chino boundary: 12 elementary, 2 middle and 2 high. They belong to Chino Valley Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Chino?

Chino has 36 parks. The largest are Prado Regional Park (about 258 acres), Ruben S Ayala Park (about 114 acres) and Mill Creek Wetlands (about 91 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Chino?

Chino is in San Bernardino County. The closest cities I cover are Chino Hills, Eastvale, Ontario and Montclair.
