The Story of
San Clemente.

San Clemente is a city of about 62,627 people in Orange County.

For green space, San Clemente has 14 parks, led by Richard T. Steed Memorial Park at roughly 20 acres. It has ocean beach: The Pier Beach, T Street Beach and North Beach. Golfers have Talega Golf Club and San Clemente Municipal Golf Course.

Local draws include Casa Romantica Cultural Center and Gardens. The map shows 132 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Pizza and Coffee the most common. There are 10 public schools inside the boundary, run by Capistrano Unified. On price, San Clemente is the 11th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.75M for the typical home, and values are up 122% in ten years.

San Clemente Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in San Clemente, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in San Clemente is worth about $1,751,315, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in San Clemente going up or down?

The typical home value in San Clemente is up 5.9% over the past year, from about $1,653,129 to about $1,751,315. Over ten years it is up 122%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in San Clemente?

Typical values in San Clemente by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $731,059, 2 bedroom about $1,206,775, 3 bedroom about $1,562,156, 4 bedroom about $1,982,141, 5+ bedroom about $2,489,951. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in San Clemente?

The typical asking rent in San Clemente is about $3,411 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in San Clemente?

10 public schools sit inside the San Clemente boundary: 6 elementary, 3 middle and 1 high. They belong to Capistrano Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in San Clemente?

San Clemente has 14 parks. The largest are Richard T. Steed Memorial Park (about 20 acres), Talega Park (about 11 acres) and Bonita Canyon Park (about 8.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near San Clemente?

San Clemente is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are San Juan Capistrano, Dana Point, Ladera Ranch and Laguna Niguel.
