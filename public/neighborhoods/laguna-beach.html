The Story of
Laguna Beach.

Laguna Beach, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 22,564 residents.

It has been its own community since 1887. There are 8 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Moulton Meadows Park, at about 8.4 acres. It has ocean beach: Arch Beach, Victoria Beach and Bird Rock Beach. Golfers have The Ranch at Laguna BeachGolf Course.

Places people go: Laguna Art Museum and Festival of the Arts Amphitheater. Laguna College of Art and Design is here too. The map shows 70 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, American and Mexican the most common. There are 4 public schools inside the boundary, run by Laguna Beach Unified. On price, Laguna Beach is the 3rd highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $3.04M for the typical home, and values are up 99% in ten years.

Laguna Beach Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Laguna Beach, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Laguna Beach is worth about $3,043,251, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Laguna Beach going up or down?

The typical home value in Laguna Beach is up 6.8% over the past year, from about $2,849,342 to about $3,043,251. Over ten years it is up 99%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Laguna Beach?

Typical values in Laguna Beach by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $1,696,683, 2 bedroom about $2,226,833, 3 bedroom about $3,149,007, 4 bedroom about $4,462,410, 5+ bedroom about $6,495,400. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Laguna Beach?

The typical asking rent in Laguna Beach is about $5,519 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Laguna Beach?

4 public schools sit inside the Laguna Beach boundary: 2 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Laguna Beach Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Laguna Beach?

Laguna Beach has 8 parks. The largest are Moulton Meadows Park (about 8.4 acres), Alta Laguna Park (about 7.1 acres) and Lang Park (about 3.8 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Laguna Beach?

Laguna Beach is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Laguna Hills and Laguna Woods.
