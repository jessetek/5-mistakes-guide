The place

The Story of
El Monte.

El Monte is a city of about 104,639 people in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1852. For green space, El Monte has 8 parks, led by Lambert Park at roughly 9.4 acres. For a day out there is El Monte Historical Museum.

The map shows 18 places to eat and drink, with Chinese and Vietnamese the most common. There are 21 public schools inside the boundary, run by El Monte City, Mountain View Elementary and El Monte Union High. On price, El Monte is the 31st most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $752K for the typical home, and values are up 82% in ten years.

Why El Monte

What Makes El Monte
Worth Calling Home.

💵

$680K Median in LA County

Genuinely the most affordable SFR market within 30 minutes of downtown LA. First-time buyers actually have a path here.

📍

Central SGV / Downtown LA Access

10 freeway and 605 access. Downtown LA in 20 minutes. The shortest commute of any sub-$700K LA County market.

🚉

Metro Gold Line Adjacent

Future Gold Line extension expected to add stations in El Monte. Transit-oriented development potential.

👨‍👩‍👧

Strong Working-Class Community

Long-tenured residents, multi-generational households, real neighborhood identity. El Monte isn't a flip market.
