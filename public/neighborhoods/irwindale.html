The Story of
Irwindale.

About 1,459 people call Irwindale home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1860. There are 4 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Santa Fe Dam Recreation Area, at about 1,396 acres.

For a day out there is Toyota Speedway at Irwindale. There are 2 public schools inside the boundary, run by Covina-Valley Unified. On price, Irwindale is the 34th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $767K for the typical home, and values are up 94% in ten years.

What is the typical home value in Irwindale, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Irwindale is worth about $767,395, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Irwindale going up or down?

The typical home value in Irwindale is up 1.2% over the past year, from about $758,388 to about $767,395. Over ten years it is up 94%.

How many public schools are in Irwindale?

2 public schools sit inside the Irwindale boundary: 1 elementary and 1 high. They belong to Covina-Valley Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Irwindale?

Irwindale has 4 parks. The largest are Santa Fe Dam Recreation Area (about 1,396 acres), KARE Youth League (about 14 acres) and Irwindale Park (about 9.9 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

Irwindale is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Baldwin Park, Vincent, Bradbury and Azusa.
