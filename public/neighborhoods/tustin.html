The place

The Story of
Tustin.

About 78,863 people call Tustin home. It sits in Orange County.

There are 21 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Veterans Sports Park, at about 32 acres. Golfers have Tustin Ranch Golf Club. Local draws include AMC Tustin 14.

Kairos Pacific University of California, Campus of AEU is here too. The map shows 156 places to eat and drink, with Coffee, Mexican and Sushi the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by Tustin Unified. On price, Tustin is the 34th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.15M for the typical home, and values are up 94% in ten years.

Why Tustin

What Makes Tustin
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🎓

Tustin Unified School District

TUSD ranks well. Beckman, Foothill, and Tustin High all have strong feeders. Schools drive Tustin's family-buyer demand.

🏘️

Tustin Ranch Master-Planned Community

Tustin Ranch was built in the 80s as one of OC's premier master-planned developments. Curving streets, top elementary feeders, mature landscaping.

🛍️

Old Town Tustin Walkable District

Real historic downtown with restaurants, the Tustin Brewing Co, and First Street's antique row. Few central OC cities have this.

📍

Central OC Location

5, 55, 261 toll freeways. Disneyland 15 min, beaches 25 min, John Wayne Airport 10 min, Irvine 5 min.
