The Story of
Pomona.

Pomona is a city of about 147,966 people in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1888. The name comes from Pomona. There are 26 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Ganesha Park, at about 61 acres. Golfers have Mountain Meadows Golf Course.

Local draws include Pomona Raceway, Wally Parks NHRA Motorsports Museum and Millard Sheets Art Center. California State Polytechnic University, Pomona is here too. The map shows 33 places to eat and drink, with American, Coffee and Breakfast the most common. There are 31 public schools inside the boundary, run by Pomona Unified. On price, Pomona is the 13th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $674K for the typical home, and values are up 92% in ten years.

Pomona Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Pomona, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Pomona is worth about $674,198, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Pomona going up or down?

The typical home value in Pomona is down 0.4% over the past year, from about $676,869 to about $674,198. Over ten years it is up 92%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Pomona?

Typical values in Pomona by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $390,781, 2 bedroom about $528,363, 3 bedroom about $674,412, 4 bedroom about $766,347, 5+ bedroom about $846,148. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Pomona?

The typical asking rent in Pomona is about $2,319 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Pomona?

31 public schools sit inside the Pomona boundary: 21 elementary, 3 middle, 5 high and 2 k to 12. They belong to Pomona Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Pomona?

Pomona has 26 parks. The largest are Ganesha Park (about 61 acres), Washington Park (about 22 acres) and Palomares Park (about 18 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Pomona?

Pomona is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are Montclair, La Verne, San Dimas and Diamond Bar.
