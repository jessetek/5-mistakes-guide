The place

The Story of
Burbank.

About 103,533 people call Burbank home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

It dates to 1887. The name comes from David Burbank. McCambridge Park, about 17 acres, is the largest of 22 parks here. Open space and trails include Wildwood Canyon Park.

Golfers have DeBell Golf Club. For a day out there is Starlight Bowl, AMC Burbank 16 and AMC Burbank Town Center 6. The map shows 205 places to eat and drink, with American, Coffee and Mexican the most common. There are 16 public schools inside the boundary, run by Burbank Unified. On price, Burbank is the 33rd highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.17M for the typical home, and values are up 86% in ten years.

Why Burbank

What Makes Burbank
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🎬

Media Industry Capital

Disney, Warner Bros, Nick, and the entire post-production ecosystem. Burbank residents often work within walking distance of major studios.

🛍️

Walkable Magnolia Park

Genuine walkable shopping district with vintage stores, restaurants, and weekly community feel.

🎓

Burbank USD Top-Tier

Burbank Unified ranks well, schools are a major demand driver, especially for the family demographic moving from Pasadena/Glendale at higher prices.

✈️

Burbank Airport Convenience

Burbank Airport (BUR) is one of LA's most convenient. 5-min drive for most residents, regional flights to NorCal/Phoenix/Vegas.
