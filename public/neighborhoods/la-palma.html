The Story of
La Palma.

La Palma, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 15,092 residents.

Its official start date is 1955, about 71 years ago. El Rancho Verde Park, about 32 acres, is the largest of 4 parks here.

The map shows 22 places to eat and drink, with Korean, Asian and Mexican the most common. There are 5 public schools inside the boundary, run by Anaheim Union High and Centralia Elementary. On price, La Palma is the 29th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.19M for the typical home, and values are up 103% in ten years.

La Palma Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in La Palma, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in La Palma is worth about $1,185,448, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in La Palma going up or down?

The typical home value in La Palma is up 4.4% over the past year, from about $1,135,038 to about $1,185,448. Over ten years it is up 103%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in La Palma?

Typical values in La Palma by bedroom count: 2 bedroom about $637,295, 3 bedroom about $1,039,298, 4 bedroom about $1,243,447, 5+ bedroom about $1,317,903. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

How many public schools are in La Palma?

5 public schools sit inside the La Palma boundary: 3 elementary, 1 middle and 1 high. They belong to Anaheim Union High and Centralia Elementary. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in La Palma?

La Palma has 4 parks. The largest are El Rancho Verde Park (about 32 acres), Central Park (about 7.5 acres) and Bettencourt Park (about 2.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near La Palma?

La Palma is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Buena Park, Cerritos, Cypress and Hawaiian Gardens.
