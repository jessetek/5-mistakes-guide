The Story of
Gardena.

Gardena, in Los Angeles County, is a city of roughly 58,921 residents.

Its official start date is 1930, about 96 years ago. For green space, Gardena has 7 parks, led by Rowley Memorial Park at roughly 18 acres. Open space and trails include Gardena Willows Wetland Preserve.

Local draws include Gardena Cinema. The map shows 58 places to eat and drink, with Japanese, Coffee and Korean the most common. There are 9 public schools inside the boundary, run by Los Angeles Unified. On price, Gardena is the 38th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $780K for the typical home, and values are up 95% in ten years.

Gardena Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in Gardena, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in Gardena is worth about $780,233, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in Gardena going up or down?

The typical home value in Gardena is up 2.2% over the past year, from about $763,358 to about $780,233. Over ten years it is up 95%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in Gardena?

Typical values in Gardena by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $389,595, 2 bedroom about $652,894, 3 bedroom about $790,576, 4 bedroom about $866,382, 5+ bedroom about $1,000,021. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in Gardena?

The typical asking rent in Gardena is about $2,334 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in Gardena?

9 public schools sit inside the Gardena boundary: 6 elementary, 1 middle and 2 high. They belong to Los Angeles Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Gardena?

Gardena has 7 parks. The largest are Rowley Memorial Park (about 18 acres), Arthur Lee Johnson Memorial Park (about 6.1 acres) and Mas Fukai Park (about 4.6 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near Gardena?

Gardena is in Los Angeles County. The closest cities I cover are West Rancho Dominguez, Lawndale, Hawthorne and Westmont.
