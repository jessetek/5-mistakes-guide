The place

The Story of
Downey.

Downey is a city of about 109,368 people in Los Angeles County.

It has been its own community since 1873. The name comes from John G. Downey. For green space, Downey has 12 parks, led by Wilderness Park at roughly 22 acres. Golfers have Los Amigos Golf Course and Rio Hondo Golf Club.

Places people go: Columbia Memorial Space Center, Stonewood Center and Cinemark Downey 14 and XD. The map shows 51 places to eat and drink, with American, Coffee and Mexican the most common. There are 19 public schools inside the boundary, run by Downey Unified. On price, Downey is the 54th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $880K for the typical home, and values are up 83% in ten years.

Why Downey

What Makes Downey
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏫

Strong School District

Downey Unified is a cornerstone of Southeast LA. Williams Elementary earns a 9/10 GreatSchools rating, and Warren and Downey High both post 97%+ graduation rates.

🏘️

Established Neighborhoods

From Old Downey to Northwest, the housing stock is mostly single-family homes from the '50s and '60s, solid construction, big lots, character.

🚗

Easy Commute

Direct freeway access via the 5, 605, and 105. Downtown LA is 20 minutes. LAX is 30. The OC line is 15.

🌮

Real Food Scene

Porto's. Mariscos Choix. The LKSD. Buena Mesa. Legit bakeries on every corner. Downey eats better than most of LA.
