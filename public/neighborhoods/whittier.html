The place

The Story of
Whittier.

Whittier is a city of about 85,589 people in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from John Greenleaf Whittier. There are 19 parks inside the boundary. The biggest is Murphy Ranch Park, at about 18 acres. Open space and trails include Puente Hills Preserve. Golfers have Friendly Hills Country Club.

Places people go: Whittier Village Cinemas. Whittier College is here too. The map shows 19 places to eat and drink, with American and Pizza the most common. There are 14 public schools inside the boundary, run by East Whittier City Elementary, Whittier City and Whittier Union High. On price, Whittier is the 45th most affordable of 122 cities on my map at about $815K for the typical home, and values are up 82% in ten years.

Why Whittier

What Makes Whittier
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏛️

Historic Charm

Uptown Whittier's tree-lined streets and craftsman homes feel like a different era. Greenleaf Avenue is one of LA's best walkable downtowns.

🎓

College Town Energy

Whittier College and Rio Hondo bring a steady mix of professors, students, and young professionals to the city.

🏞️

Hills & Trails

Friendly Hills, Murphy Ranch, and the Whittier Greenway Trail give you nature minutes from your front door.

🍷

Friday Night Scene

Whittier's downtown is alive on weekends, wine bars, breweries, and the Friday Night Concert series.
