The Story of
Temescal Valley.

Temescal Valley is a unincorporated community of about 26,232 people in Riverside County.

For green space, Temescal Valley has 4 parks, led by Deleo Regional Sports Park at roughly 38 acres. Golfers have Glen Ivy Golf Club.

For a day out there is Cowboy Gunfighters Show, Live Music and Magic Show. There are 3 public schools inside the boundary, run by Corona-Norco Unified.

How many public schools are in Temescal Valley?

3 public schools sit inside the Temescal Valley boundary: 3 elementary. They belong to Corona-Norco Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in Temescal Valley?

Temescal Valley has 4 parks. The largest are Deleo Regional Sports Park (about 38 acres), Coral Canyon Park (about 9.1 acres) and Terramor Park (about 4.8 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.
