The Story of
San Juan Capistrano.

San Juan Capistrano, in Orange County, is a city of roughly 35,469 residents.

For green space, San Juan Capistrano has 20 parks, led by San Juan Capistrano Community Center And Sports Park at roughly 19 acres. Golfers have San Juan Hills Golf Club and Marbella Country Club. For a day out there is Mission San Juan Capistrano.

The map shows 36 places to eat and drink, with Mexican, Barbecue and Indian the most common. There are 7 public schools inside the boundary, run by Capistrano Unified. On price, San Juan Capistrano is the 24th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.34M for the typical home, and values are up 100% in ten years.

San Juan Capistrano Questions,
Answered.

What is the typical home value in San Juan Capistrano, CA?

As of September 2026, the typical home in San Juan Capistrano is worth about $1,339,804, based on the Zillow Home Value Index. Prices vary a lot by street, lot size and condition, so ask for a valuation of your specific home.

Are home values in San Juan Capistrano going up or down?

The typical home value in San Juan Capistrano is up 4.2% over the past year, from about $1,285,836 to about $1,339,804. Over ten years it is up 100%.

How much is a 3-bedroom home in San Juan Capistrano?

Typical values in San Juan Capistrano by bedroom count: 1 bedroom about $421,535, 2 bedroom about $725,007, 3 bedroom about $1,089,087, 4 bedroom about $1,900,791, 5+ bedroom about $3,141,279. Source: Zillow Home Value Index, September 2026.

What is the typical rent in San Juan Capistrano?

The typical asking rent in San Juan Capistrano is about $4,164 a month as of September 2026, based on the Zillow Observed Rent Index.

How many public schools are in San Juan Capistrano?

7 public schools sit inside the San Juan Capistrano boundary: 3 elementary, 1 middle, 2 high and 1 k to 12. They belong to Capistrano Unified. School assignment depends on the home address, so confirm with the district.

What are the biggest parks in San Juan Capistrano?

San Juan Capistrano has 20 parks. The largest are San Juan Capistrano Community Center And Sports Park (about 19 acres), Historic SJC Mission City Park (about 12 acres) and C. Russell Cook Park (about 10 acres). Sizes are measured from OpenStreetMap.

What cities are near San Juan Capistrano?

San Juan Capistrano is in Orange County. The closest cities I cover are Dana Point, Ladera Ranch, Laguna Niguel and San Clemente.
