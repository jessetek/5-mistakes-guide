The place

The Story of
Torrance.

About 139,576 people call Torrance home. It sits in Los Angeles County.

The name comes from Jared Sidney Torrance. For green space, Torrance has 26 parks, led by Columbia Park at roughly 54 acres. It has ocean beach: Torrance County Beach. Open space and trails include Madrona Marsh Nature Preserve and Walteria Lake.

Places people go: AMC Rolling Hills 20 and AMC Del Amo 18. The map shows 227 places to eat and drink, with Japanese, Boba tea and Coffee the most common. There are 30 public schools inside the boundary, run by Torrance Unified. On price, Torrance is the 39th highest-priced of 122 cities on my map at about $1.1M for the typical home, and values are up 82% in ten years.

Why Torrance

What Makes Torrance
Worth Calling Home.

Photo credits

🏫

Top-Tier Schools (TUSD)

Torrance Unified is one of LA County's highest-rated districts. West HS, North HS, South HS all consistently rank 8-10/10. Buyers price in school zones aggressively.

🏝️

Beach-Adjacent Without Beach Pricing

20 minutes to Manhattan Beach, 15 to Redondo. You get South Bay coastal access at $1M-$1.5M instead of $3M+.

🍽️

Best Asian Food in LA

Mitsuwa Plaza, Gardena Ave, Sawtelle for the South Bay. Torrance has LA's deepest Japanese, Korean, and Chinese food scenes outside SGV.

💼

Toyota + Aerospace Adjacency

Torrance was Toyota North America HQ for decades; aerospace (Honeywell, Northrop) and biotech jobs drive stable demand.
