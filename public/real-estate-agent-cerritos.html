Buying a home in Cerritos

Most Cerritos buyers come for two things: the schools and a central spot between LA and Orange County. Here is how I help you get in without overpaying.

Check the district by address

Most of Cerritos is in ABC Unified, but a small western slice belongs to Bellflower Unified. Before you write an offer, I confirm the district for that exact address, so there are no surprises when school starts.

Whitney is a test, not a ZIP code

Whitney High School serves grades 7 to 12 and admits students through placement testing. Buying in Cerritos does not get your kid in by itself. If Whitney is the reason you are moving, plan for the test and look hard at Cerritos High too.

Local Knowledge

How I break down Cerritos

Cerritos is one ZIP code, 90703, but it does not shop like one. This is how I walk clients through it.

North and south of the 91: the Artesia Freeway runs straight through the middle of the city. Which side you live on shapes your commute and your errands, so we pick a side on purpose instead of by accident.

The west side along the 605: the San Gabriel River Freeway runs down the west edge of Cerritos. The small Bellflower Unified area is on the west side too, so this is where I check the school district twice.

Around the Towne Center: Cerritos Towne Center is home to the Cerritos Center for the Performing Arts, which opened in 1993. The Cerritos Millennium Library and the Sculpture Garden sit at the Civic Center nearby. Los Cerritos Center and Cerritos Auto Square cover the shopping, and the 5 touches the city's northeast border.

Parks everywhere: Don Knabe Community Regional Park has the sports complex, a skate park, pools and a lake. Heritage Park sits in the center of town, and the city keeps 18 neighborhood parks, from Bettencourt Park on Andy Street to Frontier Park on Maria Avenue, which just got a new picnic shelter.

My honest take: if schools are the top priority and the budget fits, I would start in Cerritos. If it is a stretch, look next door. Lakewood runs about $883K and Norwalk about $784K, with the same freeways close by. Fun fact for newcomers: Cerritos College is in Norwalk, not Cerritos.

One thing to do this week: pick the Cerritos street you like most and send it to me. I will pull the last six months of sales on it and tell you what a house there really costs.

FAQ

Cerritos real estate questions

How much does a home cost in Cerritos, CA?

As of October 2026, the median home price in Cerritos is about $1.06 million, and homes typically go under contract in about 31 days. Price still moves a lot with the floor plan, the remodel and the lot. I can give you a valuation for your exact street at no cost.

Does living in Cerritos get my child into Whitney High School?

No. Whitney High School is part of ABC Unified, serves grades 7 to 12, and admits students through placement testing. A Cerritos address does not get your child in by itself. Check the current rules with ABC Unified directly, and treat Cerritos High and the other district schools as part of your plan.

Which school district is my Cerritos home in?

Most of Cerritos is in the ABC Unified School District. A small western portion is in Bellflower Unified. Do not assume from the city name. I confirm the district for the exact address before you write an offer.

Should I buy in Cerritos or a city next door?

If schools and a central location matter most and the budget fits, Cerritos is hard to beat. If $1.06 million is a stretch, Lakewood runs about $883K and Norwalk about $784K as of October 2026, with the 91 and 605 close by. I can tour all three with you in one afternoon.
