Downey vs Whittier: where should you buy?

Prices, payments, schools, commute and the trade offs, from someone who sells homes in both cities every month.

By Jesse Oñate, Realtor · Updated November 13, 2026

The short version
  • Downey's median sale price is about $874K versus about $894K in Whittier, a gap of about $20K (Redfin, three months ending October 2026).
  • With 20% down at 7.11%, that gap is roughly $107 a month in principal and interest, with Downey the lower one.
  • Downey wins on freeway access and the Metro C Line. Whittier wins on hills, trails and a walkable Uptown.
  • Schools are set by the exact address in both cities, so check the boundary before you write an offer.
  • Both cities are mostly older housing stock, so the inspection matters more than the city.

Downey vs Whittier comes down to this: Downey now costs a little less and is easier to get around, while Whittier costs a little more and feels more like a small town with hills and a real downtown. Both are good places to own a home, and I sell in both every month, so I am not trying to push you one way.

If your commute runs through the 5, 105 or 710, or you want the Metro C Line, I would start with Downey. If you want a historic core, trails and a small town feel, I would start with Whittier. Here is how I break it down for clients.

How do home prices compare in Downey and Whittier?

Redfin puts the median sale price at about $874K in Downey and about $894K in Whittier for the three months ending October 2026. The median home sold in 38 days in Downey and 44 days in Whittier, so neither city is a fire sale and neither one is sitting for months.

Medians hide a lot. Whittier runs from starter homes in the Hadley area to much larger homes in Friendly Hills, and Downey runs from condos near the 105 to big lots in Northeast Downey. Compare the street you can afford, not the city average.

What is the monthly payment difference?

Freddie Mac's survey put the 30 year fixed rate at 7.11% on November 12, 2026. At that rate, with 20% down, a median priced Downey home works out to roughly $4,706 a month in principal and interest, and a median priced Whittier home to roughly $4,813. That is about $107 a month apart, before taxes and insurance.

Property tax follows the price. LA County bases the tax on 1% of assessed value plus voter approved debt, which the county says generally stays under about 1.25% in most of the county. On a $20K price difference, that is roughly $200 to $250 more per year in Whittier. Run your own numbers in my mortgage calculator and change the price, down payment and rate to see what moves the payment most.

Which city has the better commute?

Downey. It sits between the 5, 605, 105 and 710, and the Metro C Line stops at Lakewood Boulevard in the middle of the 105. That gives you real options for downtown LA, Long Beach, the South Bay and Orange County.

Whittier is no slouch, but it is a different setup. The 605 runs along the northern end of the city, State Route 72 runs along Whittier Boulevard, and there is no rail service inside the city, only bus lines. If you drive to Orange County or the San Gabriel Valley, Whittier works well. If you commute west or south every day, drive it at rush hour before you buy. I tell every client that, no matter which city they pick.

How do the schools compare?

Downey has its own district, Downey Unified, with about 22 schools. My Downey guide lists Warren High School and Downey High School among the main high schools.

Whittier is more complicated, and that matters when you buy. Several districts serve the city, including Whittier City, East Whittier City and the Whittier Union High School District, which runs Whittier, California, Santa Fe, Pioneer and La Serna High Schools. Whittier Union also covers parts of Downey, so do not assume an address in one city means one school. School assignment follows the exact address in both cities. Confirm it with the district before you write an offer, not after.

What is it like to live in each city?

Downey is practical and central. It has its own downtown on Downey Avenue, Stonewood Center and Downey Landing for shopping, and a lot of mid century ranch homes on quiet streets. I wrote a longer look at what living in Downey is really like.

Whittier is more of a college town feel. The city was incorporated in 1898, Whittier College has been here since the 1890s, and Uptown Whittier along Greenleaf Avenue is one of the more walkable shopping and dining areas in this part of LA County. The Hadley-Greenleaf area is one of the city's designated historic districts, which means a lot of character homes. It also means older homes, so inspections matter.

Which has the older homes and bigger surprises?

Both. Older homes are common in both cities, and Downey's mid century ranch homes are the clearest example. I see the same inspection items in both: old galvanized or cast iron plumbing, outdated electrical panels, sewer laterals, termite damage and foundations. In Whittier, the 1987 Whittier Narrows earthquake damaged many older buildings in Uptown, a reminder to ask about foundation bolting and retrofits on any older home.

Budget for repairs in either city and make the inspection a real conversation, not a formality. That is more important than the city you pick.

Who should buy in Downey and who should buy in Whittier?

  • Buy in Downey if freeway access, the Metro C Line, and a central location matter most, or you want your own school district. Right now it is also the slightly lower priced of the two.
  • Buy in Whittier if you want a historic downtown, hills and trails, and a small town feel.
  • Cannot decide? Pick the commute first. You will feel that every single day, while the extra bedroom or the nicer kitchen is a one time decision.

One thing to do this week

Pick two or three homes in each city, drive from each one to your work at the time you actually commute, and run the payment for both. It takes one evening, and it settles most Downey vs Whittier debates. Then send me your top picks and I will tell you what I would offer and what I would look out for.

Questions people ask me

Is Downey or Whittier more expensive?

Whittier, by a little. Redfin shows a median sale price of about $894K in Whittier and about $874K in Downey for the three months ending October 2026, about $20K apart. Individual streets in both cities can be well above or below those medians.

Which city has the better commute, Downey or Whittier?

Downey for most commutes because it sits between the 5, 605, 105 and 710 and has a Metro C Line station on Lakewood Boulevard. Whittier works well for the 605 and Orange County, but it has no rail inside the city.

Are the schools better in Downey or Whittier?

It depends on the exact address. Downey has its own district, while several districts serve Whittier and Whittier Union High School District also covers parts of Downey. Confirm the school assignment for the specific home with the district before you make an offer.

Are homes older in Downey or Whittier?

Older homes are common in both cities. Plan for plumbing, electrical, sewer and foundation checks in either one, and get a thorough inspection.

How different is the monthly payment in Downey and Whittier?

At the 7.11% Freddie Mac rate from November 12, 2026, with 20% down, a median priced Whittier home is roughly $107 a month higher in principal and interest than a median priced Downey home, before taxes and insurance.
