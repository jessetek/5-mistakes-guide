Best neighborhoods in Downey, by budget

What your money buys in South, Central and Northeast Downey, from a Downey realtor who sells here every month.

By Jesse Oñate, Realtor · Updated November 18, 2026

The short version
  • Median sale price is around $874K and homes sell in about 38 days.
  • Under $600K means condos and townhomes, mostly in South Downey (90242).
  • $750K to $900K buys 2 and 3 bedroom ranch homes, mostly in Central Downey (90241).
  • $1M and up means 4 and 5 bedroom homes on larger lots, mostly in Northeast Downey (90240).
  • Check the school boundary and the age of the plumbing before you fall in love with a block.

The best neighborhoods in Downey depend on your budget more than on a map. Downey has three ZIP codes (90240, 90241 and 90242), and the same dollar amount buys very different things in each: a condo near the 105, a mid century three bedroom in the middle of town, or a larger house on a bigger lot in the northeast. I sell homes here every month, so here is how I sort it by what you can spend.

The quick version: under about $600K you are looking at condos and townhomes, around $750K to $900K is a two or three bedroom house, and above $1M you are shopping for four bedrooms and larger lots. My Downey market guide puts the median sale price around $874K, with homes taking about 38 days to sell, so a typical buyer lands in the middle tier.

Where can I buy in Downey for under $600K?

This is the condo and townhome tier, and South Downey (90242) is where I start the search. The area sits along the 105 and the Metro C Line, and the Lakewood Boulevard station gives you a rail ride toward Norwalk and the 105 corridor. If your commute is the priority, this tier is the most practical in the city.

Two cautions. Condos come with an HOA, and the monthly dues count against your loan approval, so ask for the budget and reserve study early. Also stand outside at 5pm. Streets close to the freeways can be loud, and a quiet unit on the back side of a complex is worth more than a nicer kitchen on the front. First time buyers should read my first time buyer guide for Downey, because programs and loan limits matter most at this price.

What are the best Downey neighborhoods between $750K and $900K?

This is the heart of the market: two and three bedroom ranch homes, mostly in the central part of the city around ZIP 90241. These are the tree lined streets close to downtown Downey, with yards big enough for kids and a dog. A typical three bedroom runs about $870K, and a typical two bedroom about $743K.

Why I like it: you are close to Furman Park and Apollo Park, both off Rives Avenue. The city lists Furman Park with ball diamonds, tennis courts, a playground, picnic shelters and an activity room. Apollo Park has open green space, a soccer field, a basketball court and a playground. Independence Park, off Bellflower Boulevard, has courts for tennis and pickleball that the city rents by the hour. On a Saturday, that is where your neighbors are.

What to watch: much of this housing was built in the 1940s and 1950s. Plumbing, electrical panels, sewer lines and foundations are where inspections find surprises, so budget for them. I walk through what that looks like in my guide on living in Downey.

Which Downey neighborhoods work for $1M and up?

For four bedrooms (about $1.01M typical) and five or more bedrooms (about $1.36M), I look at Northeast Downey, ZIP 90240. Lots tend to be larger, and you see more custom builds and full remodels. This is the part of the city people usually mean when they ask me for the nicest area of Downey.

It is also where I see the most overpaying. A remodeled house at the top of the range can look like a bargain next to a similar house in a nearby city, so compare it against recent sales on the same block, not the listing price. If you are also weighing a nearby city, my comparison of Downey and Whittier shows how the two stack up on price and lifestyle.

What about schools and boundaries?

Downey Unified is its own district, and my market guide counts 19 public schools inside the city boundary, including Downey High School and Warren High School. School boundaries can change, so I always verify the assigned school for a specific address with the district before my clients count on it. Never rely on a listing for that.

What will it cost to own in each area?

Property taxes do not change much between neighborhoods. LA County says the base rate is 1% of assessed value plus voter approved bonds, so on an $870K purchase expect roughly $10,000 to $11,000 a year. The bigger swing is the HOA on a condo and the repair budget on an older house. Special assessments such as Mello Roos are less common in older parts of Downey, but I still check every disclosure. My Mello Roos guide shows how to spot them.

Renting is not cheap either. My guide shows typical asking rent around $2,257 a month in September 2026, so run the numbers in the rent vs buy calculator before deciding.

How I would pick a Downey neighborhood this week

  1. Set your real monthly payment first, then work backward to the price.
  2. Pick your tier from the table above and ignore the other two.
  3. Visit your top two blocks on a weekday at 5pm and on a Saturday morning.
  4. Walk to the nearest park. If you will not use it, the block is wrong for you.
  5. Check the school boundary for the exact address with the district.

Questions people ask me

What is the nicest neighborhood in Downey?

Most people point to Northeast Downey, ZIP 90240, for larger lots and remodeled homes. Nicest still depends on budget. For a 3 bedroom near parks, I often steer clients to Central Downey, ZIP 90241.

Where should a first time buyer look in Downey?

Start with condos and townhomes in South Downey, ZIP 90242, where typical condo values are around $543K. Then compare a 2 bedroom house, which runs about $743K. My first time buyer page for Downey covers programs.

How much is a 3 bedroom house in Downey?

A typical 3 bedroom in Downey is about $870K, based on my Downey market guide for September 2026. Condition, lot size and street move that number a lot.

Is South Downey a good area to buy?

It can be, especially if you want freeway and Metro C Line access and a lower entry price. Check HOA dues on condos and stand outside at rush hour to judge freeway noise.

Do property taxes differ between Downey neighborhoods?

The base rate is the same across LA County, 1% of assessed value plus voter approved bonds. The difference between areas comes from HOA dues, repairs and any special assessments in the disclosures.
