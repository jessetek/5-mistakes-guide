Buying a home in Montebello

Montebello is one ZIP code, 90640, home to about 62K people, with the Pomona Freeway running straight through town. Here is how I help buyers get in without overpaying.

Comps from your block

Montebello prices change from one street to the next. Before you write an offer, I pull what actually closed nearby so you bid with a number, not a feeling.

Check the ground, too

Standard Oil struck oil in the Montebello hills in 1917, and the hills filled with wells for decades. For homes near the hills, I look the parcel up on the state's CalGEM Well Finder map before your contingencies run out.

Know your commute

The Montebello/Commerce Metrolink stop runs Riverside Line trains to Union Station, but only on weekdays and mostly at peak hours. If you plan to ride it, we check the schedule against your job first.

First-time buyer help

FHA, CalHFA and other down payment programs can cover more than most people think. Pair one with a lender who closes on time and an $852K median gets a lot more reachable.

Local Knowledge

Montebello, the parts a listing leaves out

The things I point out on a tour that you will never find in the listing description.

Schools: Montebello Unified School District serves the city, and the two public high schools buyers ask me about most are Montebello High School and Schurr High School. Boundaries do not follow the ZIP code, so I confirm the assigned school for the exact address with the district before you write an offer.

Parks and the golf course: Grant Rea Park and Montebello City Park carry most of the weekend sports. The Montebello Municipal Golf Course is an 18-hole course that opened as a private club in 1928 and has been city owned since 1941. If green space is high on your list, we start the search on the streets around it.

History you can still see: Montebello incorporated in 1920. Bicknell Park holds the Armenian Genocide Martyrs Monument, the Juan Matias Sanchez Adobe from the 1840s still stands in its own park, and a state landmark at Bluff Road and Washington Boulevard marks the site of the Battle of Rio San Gabriel.

Getting around: the city runs its own bus system, Montebello Bus Lines, which started in 1931 with four buses. The Shops at Montebello is the main shopping center and one of the city's biggest employers. If you want to skip the car for errands, those routes matter.

Next door: a lot of my Montebello buyers also look in Pico Rivera and Whittier, and I work both. If the right house turns out to be one city over, I will tell you.
