For Buyers

Buying a home in Bellflower

If Long Beach or Lakewood priced you out, Bellflower is often where the math starts working again. Here's how I help Bellflower buyers get in without buying a problem.

Know which district you're in

Most of Bellflower is in Bellflower Unified, which runs Bellflower High and Mayfair High. Mayfair's campus is actually across the line in Lakewood, on Woodruff Avenue. Some Bellflower streets fall into Downey Unified or Paramount Unified, so I check every address.

Look at duplexes, too

Bellflower has a lot of older duplexes and small apartment buildings. FHA loans can be used on a 2 to 4 unit property you live in, so renting out the other unit can help cover the payment. I'll run the rent math with you before you offer.

Inspect for age

A big share of Bellflower's homes went up in the 1940s and 1950s. Sewer lines, electrical panels and old room additions are where the surprises hide. I make sure your inspections cover them before your contingency runs out.

Local Knowledge

Bellflower, side by side

Bellflower calls itself "The Friendly City." It was dairy country first, settled by Dutch, Japanese and Portuguese farmers, and incorporated in 1957. After the war, many of those dairies moved east to Dairy Valley, the town we now call Cerritos. Here's how the city breaks down today.

Downtown, along Bellflower Boulevard: the city's main shopping street since the 1950s. Metro's planned Southeast Gateway Line is set to stop at the old Pacific Electric station here, with service targeted around 2035. It's a plan, not a promise, but it's worth knowing if you're buying near downtown. John S. Simms Park, with its fishing pond, is close by.

The east side, by the San Gabriel River: home to Ruth R. Caruthers Park, a 20-acre park with a skate park and lighted ball fields. The Bellflower Bike Trail starts there and runs about 2.7 miles along the old Pacific Electric right-of-way, connecting to the San Gabriel River Trail.

The south side, along the 91: the Artesia Freeway runs east to west through the southern part of town, which is handy if you work in Orange County or head toward Long Beach. Lakewood starts just south of here.

The north side, toward Downey: the I-105 runs just north of the city and the I-605 just east of it, so the north end is the quickest jump onto the 105 heading west toward the airport side of LA County.

Where your budget fits: condos and townhomes are usually the lowest way in. A detached 1950s house is the next step up and the most common home in town. If you want help with the payment, a duplex you live in is worth a serious look here. For families, Thompson Park has ball fields and pools, one of three supervised city parks along with Caruthers and Simms.

FAQ

Bellflower real estate questions

How much does a home cost in Bellflower, CA?

The median Bellflower home is about $680,000, and homes take around 50 days to go under contract. That's well below Lakewood and Cerritos. Condos and townhomes come in lower, and duplexes are priced partly on their rents. For your exact property, I'll do a free valuation based on your street and floor plan.

Which school district serves Bellflower homes?

Most of Bellflower is in Bellflower Unified, which runs Bellflower High and Mayfair High (Mayfair's campus is in Lakewood). Parts of the city are served by Downey Unified and Paramount Unified. Check by address, not by ZIP. I confirm the boundary before you write an offer.

Can I buy a duplex in Bellflower and live in one unit?

Yes. Bellflower has plenty of older duplexes, and FHA financing can be used on a 2 to 4 unit property you live in. The rent from the other unit can help with the payment. Talk to your lender about how much of that rent counts toward qualifying, and to a CPA about the tax side.

Is a Metro rail line coming to Bellflower?

It's planned. Metro's Southeast Gateway Line is set to include a Bellflower stop at the former Pacific Electric station downtown, with service targeted around 2035. Big transit timelines often move, so I'd treat it as a possible bonus, not the reason to buy.
