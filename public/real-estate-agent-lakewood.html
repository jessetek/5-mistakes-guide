For Buyers

Buying a home in Lakewood

At about 35 days on market, good Lakewood homes do not wait around. Be ready before you tour.

Check the school district first

Lakewood is split between Long Beach Unified, Bellflower Unified and ABC Unified, with a small piece in Paramount Unified. Two houses a few blocks apart can feed different districts. I confirm the district for every address before you fall for it.

Same plan, different value

When the house down the street has the same layout, the comp is easy to find. The hard part is adjusting for what changed: permitted additions, updated electrical and plumbing, a converted garage. I check permits so you pay for the house that's really there.

Inspect a 1950s house like one

Many Lakewood homes date to the early 1950s. I'll push for a sewer line scope and a close look at the electrical panel, roof and any older add-ons, so you negotiate repairs with facts.

For Sellers

Selling your Lakewood home

Buyers here compare your house to the same floor plan two streets over. Your job is to be the obvious best one.

Priced against your twin

I find the recent sales of homes most like yours, then price for the differences buyers will actually pay for. Not a Zestimate, and not a number picked to win your listing.

Show what's been done

New roof, updated panel, finished permits, a remodeled bath. In a tract city, the upgrades are the story. I put them up front with receipts and permits ready, so buyers trust the price.

Fix only what pays

Fresh paint, clean landscaping and small repairs often pay for themselves. A big remodel right before listing usually does not. I'll tell you which is which for your house before you spend.

Local Knowledge

Lakewood, the parts that matter

Lakewood incorporated on April 16, 1954, and it still runs much the way it was planned: parks spread through the neighborhoods, Lakewood Boulevard down the middle, and the 605 along the east side.

Around Mayfair Park: Mayfair Park, at Clark Avenue and South Street in the center of the city, has been Lakewood's flagship park since 1951. Homes within walking distance of a good park are an easy sell here, and buyers notice.

Near Lakewood Center and Del Amo: Lakewood Center opened in 1951 at Lakewood Boulevard and Del Amo Boulevard, and it is still the city's main shopping hub. Bolivar Park, with McCormick Pool, sits at Del Amo and Downey Avenue. This central stretch puts you within a couple of miles of the 91.

South Lakewood, by the golf course: Lakewood Country Club is a public 18-hole course on Carson Street, right next to Long Beach Airport. Streets near it trade on the open space. Ask about airport noise for any home under the flight path, and hear it for yourself before you write an offer.

The east side, near the 605: the San Gabriel River Freeway runs through Lakewood's eastern side, so these streets are the quickest commute toward Orange County. Del Valle Park, home of the Lakewood Youth Center and summer concerts, is a local favorite.

Schools families ask about: Lakewood High School (Long Beach Unified), Mayfair High School (Bellflower Unified) and Cleveland Elementary. Always verify the assigned school for a specific address with the district.

Shopping nearby cities too? One trap: Lakewood Village is not in Lakewood. It was annexed into Long Beach, so those homes have Long Beach city services. See my Long Beach page, plus my Cerritos guide and Bellflower guide if you're comparing.

Lakewood real estate questions

How much does a home cost in Lakewood, CA?

The Lakewood median is around $883K, with homes averaging about 35 days on market, per my 2026 Lakewood guide. Size, upgrades, permits and school district move the number a lot, even between similar floor plans. For your exact house, start with a free valuation.

What school district is Lakewood in?

More than one. Lakewood is served mainly by Long Beach Unified, Bellflower Unified and ABC Unified, with a small part in Paramount Unified. Lakewood High School is in Long Beach Unified and Mayfair High School is in Bellflower Unified. Confirm the district for each address before you make an offer.

Is Lakewood Village part of Lakewood?

No. Lakewood Village was annexed into Long Beach, so homes there are in the City of Long Beach even though the name says Lakewood. If city services or taxes matter to your decision, check the city limits on every listing. I do it for every home we look at.

How fast do homes sell in Lakewood?

About 35 days, per my 2026 guide, which is faster than Long Beach. Clean, well-priced homes often go quicker. If you're buying, get pre-approved before you tour. If you're selling, price right in week one.
