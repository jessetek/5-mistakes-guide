The city has roots most people never hear about. In 1888, Chicago mapmaker Andrew McNally bought more than 2,200 acres of Rancho Los Coyotes and built Windermere Ranch, ringed with olive, orange and lemon groves. That ranch site is Neff Park today. Local Knowledge

How I break down La Mirada

One ZIP code, 90638, but the north side, the parks and the freeway edges each buy differently.

Creek Park and the north side: Creek Park and Granada Heights are in northern La Mirada. La Mirada Creek Park and Oak Creek Park follow the creek, and Oak Creek's entrance sits off La Mirada Boulevard across from Biola University. Foster Park, home to St. Paul of the Cross School, is another name you will see on listings.

Around "The Grove": locals call La Mirada Community Regional Park on Adelfa Avenue "The Grove." It sits next to the county's 18-hole La Mirada Golf Course and has disc golf that dates back to 1976. Splash! La Mirada Regional Aquatics Center and the La Mirada Theatre for the Performing Arts are the other big draws for families.

The freeway edges: the 5 only clips the southwest corner of the city, and Beach Boulevard runs briefly through the east end. Imperial Highway is the main east to west road. If you commute every day, how close you live to the 5 changes your morning, so we time the drive before you offer.

My honest take: if you want a quieter suburb with big parks and one clear public high school, La Mirada is a strong pick at about $830K. If you need a little more room in the budget, La Habra runs about $815K. Whittier sits close at about $847K with more variety in home styles. Cerritos is about $1.05M.

One thing to do this week: drive the street you like at 5pm on a weekday, then send me the address. I will tell you what the last few sales on that block really closed for.

La Mirada real estate questions

How much does a home cost in La Mirada, CA?

As of 2026, the median home price in La Mirada is about $830,000, and homes typically go under contract in about 42 days. Starter homes can draw several offers, while homes priced above the comps tend to sit. I can give you a valuation for your exact street at no cost.

What school district is La Mirada in?

Most of La Mirada is in the Norwalk-La Mirada Unified School District. La Mirada High School is the city's one public high school. Elementary assignments depend on the address, so confirm with the district for any home you are serious about.

Can a first-time buyer still win in La Mirada?

Yes, with the right strategy. In March 2026 my client Aaron, a first-time buyer with 3.5% down on an FHA loan, won a La Mirada home against five competing offers at $18K under list. We found the house other buyers passed on and solved the seller's real problem instead of bidding higher.

Is La Mirada a good place to live if I commute?

It depends on where in the city you buy. The 5 only touches the southwest corner, Beach Boulevard crosses the east end, and Imperial Highway is the main road through town. Drive your route at rush hour before you make an offer.
