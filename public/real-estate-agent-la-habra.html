Buying a home in La Habra

La Habra is about 62K people in the north corner of Orange County, right on the LA County line. Here is how I help buyers get in without overpaying or getting surprised after escrow opens.

Check the city, not just the ZIP

90631 covers La Habra in Orange County and La Habra Heights in LA County. Same ZIP, different city, county and rules. I confirm which side a listing sits on before you fall for it.

Schools split by grade

La Habra City School District covers elementary and middle school, and Fullerton Joint Union High School District runs La Habra High and Sonora High. Some addresses fall in other districts, so I verify the exact house.

No freeway in town

La Habra has no freeway inside city limits. Most commuters take Imperial Highway east to the 57, or ride Beach Boulevard or Harbor Boulevard south. Drive your route at rush hour before you buy.

Local Knowledge

What La Habra locals already know

The stuff I bring up on a showing that never makes it into the listing.

Where the name comes from: La Habra means the pass through the hills, after the natural gap in the Puente Hills. The land was granted as Rancho Cañada de La Habra in 1839, the city incorporated in 1925, and by 1928 it was the biggest avocado center in Southern California.

The main roads: three state routes run through town as regular streets: Whittier Boulevard (SR 72), Beach Boulevard (SR 39) and Imperial Highway (SR 90). Idaho Street, Euclid Street and Palm Street carry north and south traffic, with La Habra Boulevard and Lambert Road running east and west. Which of those your street feeds into decides how your mornings go.

Family spots: the Children's Museum at La Habra lives in a 1923 Union Pacific depot on Euclid Street, with a historic caboose outside. La Bonita Park on Whittier Boulevard covers 22 acres with a skate park, courts and softball fields.

Corn Festival weekend: every August the La Habra Host Lions Club throws the Corn Festival, with a Saturday morning parade that ranks among the oldest in Southern California. The money pays for eye exams and glasses for students who need them. If you are new to town, go.

Next door: plenty of my La Habra buyers also tour La Mirada and Fullerton, and I work both. If the right house is one city over, I will tell you.

Is La Habra in Orange County or LA County?

La Habra is in Orange County. The mix-up comes from La Habra Heights, a separate city in LA County that shares the 90631 ZIP. Always check the city on the listing and on the county assessor record before you write an offer.

What schools serve La Habra homes?

Most homes are in La Habra City School District for elementary and middle school and Fullerton Joint Union High School District for high school, which runs La Habra High and Sonora High. Some addresses are served by other districts, so confirm the assignment for the exact house with the district.
