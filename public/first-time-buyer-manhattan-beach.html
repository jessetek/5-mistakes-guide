Buying your first home in Manhattan Beach.

Manhattan Beach's median is $3.15M, with sub-area variation across The Strand, Tree Section, Sand Section, Hill Section. Manhattan Beach USD. The right neighborhood depends on your budget, your commute, and what you actually want, not what Zillow shows you first.

Step 1: Get pre-approved

Pre-approval letter dictates everything downstream. I introduce you to bilingual lenders who specialize in first-time buyers. At Manhattan Beach prices most first-time programs cap out, so we focus on high-balance and jumbo options that actually fit.

Step 2: Know the Manhattan Beach traps

Supplemental taxes, HOA dues, transfer fees, and Mello-Roos in newer developments. Most agents skip these. I show you the full monthly cost before you write the offer.

Step 3: Tour the right neighborhoods

Manhattan Beach sub-areas: The Strand, Tree Section, Sand Section, Hill Section. Each has a different price ceiling and lifestyle. We walk together so you see what's realistic vs aspirational.
