Local Knowledge

Whittier, area by area

Whittier was founded by Quakers and incorporated in 1898. The age of a street tells you a lot about the house on it.

Uptown and the historic districts. Uptown, centered on Philadelphia Street and Greenleaf Avenue, is the old downtown. Just north, the Hadley-Greenleaf and Central Park historic districts are full of Craftsman and other early 1900s homes, and College Hills wraps around Whittier College. Buyers pay for that character. Historic district rules can limit exterior changes, so I check before you plan a remodel.

Friendly Hills, Murphy Ranch and Leffingwell Ranch. Mostly built in the 1950s and 1960s. Friendly Hills and Murphy Ranch sit north of Whittier Boulevard toward the hills, and Leffingwell Ranch is just south of it. Larger lots, and views on the Friendly Hills streets that climb toward the hills, put this side of town near the top of the Whittier price range.

East Whittier. East of College Avenue, this was farmland until after World War II. Today it is mostly postwar homes, with shopping at the Whittwood Town Center. For a lot of families, this is where the first Whittier offer gets written.

Trails and open space. Hellman Park at the top of Greenleaf Avenue and the Turnbull Canyon trailhead off Turnbull Canyon Road lead into the Puente Hills preserve. The Whittier Greenway Trail runs about four and a half miles across town on an old Union Pacific rail line. Homes within a short walk of it get noticed.

Commute. Whittier Boulevard (State Route 72) is the main spine through town, and the 605 is your link to the 5, the 60 and the 10. If you drive to downtown LA daily, test the route at rush hour before you commit to the far east side.

Older homes and earthquakes. The 1987 Whittier Narrows earthquake damaged many historic Uptown buildings. If you are buying a pre-war home, a foundation inspection and a check for bolting is money well spent.

Whittier close but not quite right? Compare La Mirada and La Habra, two neighbors I cover the same way. One thing to do this week: drive your top two Whittier areas at 5pm on a weekday.
